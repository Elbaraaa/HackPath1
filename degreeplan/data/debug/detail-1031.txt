ANTH460 - History of Archaeological Theory
Anthropology, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
007109
Course Description
Explores the relationship between method and theory in anthropological archaeology over the past 100 years. The intimate relationship between general theory and the development of methods and research interests in archaeology will be demonstrated through case studies.
