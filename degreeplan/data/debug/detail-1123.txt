ANTH545A - Contemporary Approaches to Human Biology
Anthropology, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
007257
Course Description
Study of humans using biocultural perspective. Emphasis on human growth and development and human population ecology. Includes application of method and theory in the analysis of nutritional, disease and lifestyle factors. Involves scholarly exchange and secondary research. Graduate-level requirements include additional readings and a research paper.
