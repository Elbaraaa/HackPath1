Anthropology, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course offers an anthropological and historical perspective on credit, examining how it is culturally embedded in social relations and used as an instrument of power from ancient societies to capitalist systems, and covers topics from consumer credit to World Bank and IMF structural adjustment loans, to micro finance.
