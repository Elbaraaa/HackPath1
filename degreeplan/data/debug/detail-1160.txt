Anthropology, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Investigations of the illness experience; symbolic interpretations of medicines and medical procedures; doctor-patient communications and illness narratives. Course demonstrates the applicability of major social science theories in the related study of health-related behavior.
