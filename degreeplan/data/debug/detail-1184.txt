Anthropology, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course centers on the exchange of anthropological research through the presentation and discussion of published and on-going research. Topics of the colloquium series will be varied, with the goal of presenting the full breadth of anthropological inquiry. Graduate-level requirements include demonstration of a greater level of engagement with the colloquium presentations, including asking questions when appropriate.
