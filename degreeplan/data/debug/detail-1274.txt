Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043626
Course Description
DevOps is the combination of approaches, practices, tools and cultural philosophies that optimizes and streamlines an organization's ability to deliver services and applications at high velocity and scale. DevOps focuses on creating more value in less time by fostering collaboration between key teams and automating processes throughout the development lifecycle. In this course, you will learn to create more value in less time by integrating key teams and processes.
