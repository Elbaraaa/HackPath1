ACBS497B - Applied Animal Behavior Workshop
Animal & Biomedical Sciences  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041114
Course Description
The course will provide the opportunity for applying the principles from the discipline of applied animal behavior to a variety of real-world captive settings (e.g., companion, farm, zoo, laboratory) through illustrative case studies, observation, and literature research.
