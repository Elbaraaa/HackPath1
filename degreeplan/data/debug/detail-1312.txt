ARB427B - Colloquial Moroccan Arabic II
Global Studies, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
007516
Course Description
This is a continuation of ARB 427A-527A. The focus is on spoken rather than Standard written Arabic, and will target oral/aural skills, speaking and listening. Knowledge of Arabic orthography is not required, as the text is in phonetic transcription.
