Architecture, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Introduction to fundamental design skills found at the University level, including: understanding of ordering principles, visual analysis, site analysis, programmatic ordering, technical and freehand drawing and use of digital tools to establish a critical framework for thinking and making.
