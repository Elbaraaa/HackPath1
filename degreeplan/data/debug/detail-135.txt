ACBS498C - Applied Biotechnology Senior Capstone
Plant Sciences, Sch  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041867
Course Description
In this course, students will prepare for their future profession by exploring possible careers, creating materials to market themselves to potential employers, and improving their interview skills. They will also demonstrate their cognitive and intellectual growth in the Applied Biotechnology major by documenting and reflecting on assignments from previous courses. By the end of the course, students will have prepared an ePortfolio to showcase their accomplishments and professional potential.
