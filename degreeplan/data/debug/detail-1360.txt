Architecture, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course focuses on the role of Building Information Modeling (BIM) in Architecture and Engineering. Students will learn the fundamental processes of BIM based on 3D computer drafting, including site analysis and data extraction, basic model building, dimensioning, planning, and elevations, parametric modeling, documentation, and 3D rendering.
