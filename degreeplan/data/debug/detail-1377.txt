ARC305B - Spatial Design: Sites
Architecture, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043247
Course Description
In this intermediate level spatial design studio, students develop skills problem seeking, analyzing, iteratively speculating and developing spatial propositions, through diverse media. Students explore human scale urban/landscape environments. Research about and through design underpins propositions. Design pedagogies may draw upon programmatic/performative, site/situation-informed, contemporary theories and issues and/or other methods.
