ARC326 - Pre-Design
Architecture, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
007580
Course Description
Introduces Pre-Design approaches and strategies, including site analysis principles relating to physical, biological, cultural and regulatory attributes of a site, and architectural programming methodologies including problem seeking, goal identification, code search, observation, questioning to arrive at relationship diagrams, space allocations and simulation as techniques for processing information for building design.
