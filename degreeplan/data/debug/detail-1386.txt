ARC345A - Visualization and Fabrication
Architecture, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043202
Course Description
In this course students investigate and develop visualization and fabrication skills. These skills are developed through a series of interrelated drawing, modelling and fabrication exercises intended to instill fundamental techniques, develop methods for exploring materials, their manipulation, aggregation and installation, and build upon conceptual foundations for object design and spatial design.
