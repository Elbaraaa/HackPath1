ARC405 - Interdisciplinary Design Studio
Architecture, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043243
Course Description
This interdisciplinary design studio engages students in interdisciplinary and collaborative design in application to real world contemporary challenges facing communities near and far. Students develop skills identifying and defining design goals through engagement with communities; identify opportunities for complimentary collaborations between individuals and communities and across design media and areas of expertise; engage in research for and through design. Studios may draw from a diversity of design methodologies and pedagogies. Students develop skills iterating and communicating design propositions.
