ACBS500B - Animal Anatomy and Physiology B
Animal & Biomedical Sciences  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
033180
Course Description
This is one of two lecture courses comprising a two-semester sequence of functional animal anatomy and systemic physiology, with an emphasis on common domestic mammalian species. Some consideration is also given to what happens to the individual when form or function goes awry to illustrate the importance of the norm.
