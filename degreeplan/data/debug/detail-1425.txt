ARC487 - How Design Matters
Architecture, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course prepares students to synthesize and position their work as emerging designers through the development of a portfolio and written statement and through the formulation of a proposal for a senior capstone design project (to be realized during the
subsequent semester).
