Animal & Biomedical Sciences  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
A laboratory-based course designed to introduce basic molecular techniques, such as manipulation of DNA and RNA molecules and analysis of gene and protein expression, and their application in the experimental research laboratory.
