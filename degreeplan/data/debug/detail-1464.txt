ARC526 - Pre-Design: Analysis and Methods
Architecture, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041907
Course Description
Develops Pre-Design analysis and methods, including physical, biological, cultural, and regulatory attributes of a site. This history and practice of architectural programming includes William Pena's system of Problem Seeking, goal identification, code analysis, functional relationship modelling, space estimation, and the transformation of information into pre-design.
