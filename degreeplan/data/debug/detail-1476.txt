ARC561A - Water Efficiency in Buildings
Architecture, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039175
Course Description
Learn methods and advanced techniques that conserve urban water usage while promoting water harvesting, water reuse, and water energy generation technologies in and around buildings.

Graduate students are required to submit case studies and various calculations in each module.
