Architecture, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
035339
Course Description
A fully online course that fosters awareness and thorough understanding of the qualitative and quantifiable environmental forces that contribute to energy use in buildings. The course introduces basics for understanding solar energy and light, climate and microclimate, and human thermal comfort as related to the built environment. Graduate-level requirements include completion of an additional 2-page report to be uploaded with each skill development exercise.
