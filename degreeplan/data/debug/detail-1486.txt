Course Description
Directed research based course on emergent topics of interest to the profession. Students will develop problem-solving skills while conducting research on pre-selected topics. Graduate-level requirements include generating more complete findings on subject matter and generating publishable reports or scholarly journal articles and take a leading role in answering questions and facilitating development of research project.
