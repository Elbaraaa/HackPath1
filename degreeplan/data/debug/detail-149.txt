ACBS516 - Assisted Reproductive Technologies in Horses
Animal & Biomedical Sciences  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
006740
Course Description
Course will focus on aspects of equine reproduction, with an emphasis placed on current assisted reproductive technologies. Students will gain experience with artificial insemination practices and develop an understanding of recent, cutting-edge biotechnology. Graduate-level requirements include assisting undergraduate students.
