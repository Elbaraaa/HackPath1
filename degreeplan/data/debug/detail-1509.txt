ARC597K - The Portfolio: Advanced Graphics, Presentation, Layout, and Design
Architecture, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039264
Course Description
The development of an individual portfolio, comprised of improved past work, that demonstrates the student's philosophy, design, graphic skills, digital literacy, and technical abilities.

Graduate deliverables will be supplemented with a \"postscript reflection\" that critically examines the potential of the work to open and propel additional explorations and avenues of inquiry.
