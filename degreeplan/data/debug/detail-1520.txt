ARCE201 - Materials and Methods in Architecture
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042789
Course Description
The following course is intended to provide an overview of the materials used in building construction, their properties, and methods of fabrication and installation. Course will include lab for hands on manipulation/observation of material processes.
