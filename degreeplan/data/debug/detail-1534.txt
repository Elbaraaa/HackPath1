ARCE400B - Senior Engineering Design
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040999
Course Description
Students will use the architectural building they designed in ARC/ARCE 400A as a framework to engineer building systems including mechanical HVAC, electrical power and lighting, structural, plumbing, and fire protection. Students will apply their knowledge of architectural engineering using visioning, conceptualization, quantitative analysis, value engineering, and presenting solutions.
