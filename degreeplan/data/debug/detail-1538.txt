ARCE451 - Sustainability and Resilience for the Built Environment in the Southwest US
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043951
Course Description
The following course is intended to provide a broad Architectural Engineering perspective of the current and future challenges of designing building systems and utility infrastructure in the desert Southwest. The backdrop for topic analysis will be the University of Arizona Campus and other Southwest US Universities and Green/Sustainable Building Protocols/Rating Systems.
