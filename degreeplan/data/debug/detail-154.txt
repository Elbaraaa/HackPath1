ACBS528 - Biotechnology in Animal Sciences
Animal & Biomedical Sciences  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
006743
Course Description
Survey of principles of current protein and nucleic acid techniques useful in Animal Sciences research. This course will focus on various techniques, when to use these methods and interpretation of data from these techniques. Included will be in depth discussions on the techniques used, conclusions drawn from the experiments and planning of experiments with appropriate techniques.
