ARCE552 - Building Energy Modeling
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
044113
Course Description
The following course is intended to teach the principles and applications of building energy modeling (BEM). The course introduces physics-based, data-driven, and grey-box modeling and analysis of energy use and performance in building and HVAC&R equipment. The students will acquire skills to understand and utilize major energy simulation programs (EnergyPlus) and data science tools (Python) to solve complex real-world engineering problems in building energy efficiency and decarbonization.
