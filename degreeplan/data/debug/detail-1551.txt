Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Building science draws on foundational knowledge of many areas including architecture, structures, mechanical systems, power systems, and construction engineering/management. This course will emphasize selected topics associated with design and analysis of individual or integrated building systems. This course will draw from relevant industry sectors, building systems and building science topics. (1-3 credits, can be repeated for different topics)
