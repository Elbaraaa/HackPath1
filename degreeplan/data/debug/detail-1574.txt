ARE493B - Student Teaching in Art and Visual Culture Education
Art Education, Div  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
036092
Course Description
This course provides a student teacher with a placement in either a K-8, middle school, or high school classroom setting for integrating course content in art and visual culture education with actual practice under the supervision of a certified cooperating/mentor teacher and an AVCE university supervisor.
