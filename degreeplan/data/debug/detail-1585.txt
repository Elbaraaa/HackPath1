ARE534 - Cross-Cultural Issues in Art and Visual Culture Education
Art Education, Div  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Multicultural cross-cultural issues, gender and social justice issues within art and visual culture education. Graduate-level requirements include a choice of either developing a curriculum unit/project based on some aspect of multicultural art education, research and writing a ten-page paper related to a multicultural topic, or conducting a small multicultural research project.
