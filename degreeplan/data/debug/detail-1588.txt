ARE540 - Theories of Child Art
Art Education, Div  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Examination of contemporary theories, issues and practices related to current theories of children's development in art. Graduate-level requirements include: An in-depth research paper or project, creation and moderation of an online discussion, and higher grading standards.
