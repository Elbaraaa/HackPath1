ARE560 - Curriculum Theory in Art & Visual Culture Education
Art Education, Div  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Examination of curriculum & instruction theories, issues and practices related to art and visual culture education. Graduate-level requirements include students facilitating a one-hour class session. Graduate papers are expected to be more in-depth and scholarly. Graduate grading expectations are higher.
