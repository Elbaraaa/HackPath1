AREC101 - Markets & Morality
Political Economy & Moral Sci  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
038776
Course Description
CENTRAL QUESTIONS:
Why do some societies grow rich while other societies remain poor?
What must a person do to succeed in market society?
What must a person do to deserve to succeed? (What does it mean to truly be of service?)
How do entrepreneurs think and act? How do they start a business? How do they sustain a business? How do they learn to acknowledge and correct flaws in their business model?
