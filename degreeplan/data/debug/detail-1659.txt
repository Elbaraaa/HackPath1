Agricultural & Applied Econ  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Economics of and methods to assess household- and farm-level decision making. Students will become familiar with the microeconomic tools and statistical methods utilized to understand, evaluate, and assess empirically analyze the agricultural development challenges in lower income countries.
