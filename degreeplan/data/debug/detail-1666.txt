AREC577 - Advanced Topics in Water Resource Economics and Policy
Agricultural & Applied Econ  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008029
Course Description
Advanced economic approaches applied to water management and policy. Topics include economic modeling, pricing, incentive-based policies, regulation of pollution, alternative regulatory instruments and managing risk. Interactive seminar style course. Calculus proficiency and regular attendance essential.
