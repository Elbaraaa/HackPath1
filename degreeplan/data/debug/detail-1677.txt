Course Description
The Economics, Law and Environment workshop (ELE Workshop) is intended to expose students and participating faculty members to a broad range of original research in the area of overlap between law, economics and environmental issues. The workshop will consist of six meetings during which leading scholars in the field will present their research for discussion and critique by the enrolled students and participating faculty. The workshop is part of the broader Program on Economics, Law and the Environment, a research and educational collaboration between the College of Law and the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences.
