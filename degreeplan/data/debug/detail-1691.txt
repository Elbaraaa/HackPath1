ARH312 - Survey of Medieval Art and Architecture
Art History  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008060
Course Description
Survey of medieval Christian, Islamic and Jewish art and architecture from the late Roman Empire through the fifteenth century. Religious and secular works of art are studied within their cultural and political contexts.
