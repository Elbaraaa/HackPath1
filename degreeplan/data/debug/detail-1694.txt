ARH316A - Baroque Art and Culture
Art History  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008064
Course Description
A survey of Western Art and Architecture in seventeenth-century Europe. This lecture course will examine the major artists, artistic monuments and movements of the seventeenth-century and address the critical issues in the study of seventeenth-century art.
