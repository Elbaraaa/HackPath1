ARH400 - Topics in Museum Studies
Art History  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008089
Course Description
This course examines the theoretical and methodological issues related to museum studies. Topics will include the history of display, changing roles of museums, museums and communities, the politics of collecting, and exhibition design.
