ARH424C - History of Photography: Contemporary Trends
Art History  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008120
Course Description
This course surveys the role of photography in contemporary art. Themes include: the 'photo boom' of the 1970s, photography's relationship to other art movements, the rise of alternative and digital processes, and globalism.
