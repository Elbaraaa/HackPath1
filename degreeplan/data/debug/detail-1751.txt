Art History  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008144
Course Description
Introduction to the history and theory of connoisseurial methodologies in art collecting, museums, auction houses, and galleries. Course includes fieldwork and workshops designed to make the theory and practice of connoisseurship relevant today to the field of museum.
