ARH511B - New Theory and Methods in Art History: 1960-Present
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008161
Course Description
Second half of required theory and methods component designed to introduce participants to the impact of theory on art history post 1960. Will review representative range of art historical analyses and study recent theories of meaning and culture that have displaced these models.
