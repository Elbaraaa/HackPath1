ARH512A - Medieval Art: Empire and Periphery
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008162
Course Description
This course focuses on the art and architecture of the empires of the early Middle Ages (Roman Christian, Carolingian, Byzantine) and of the so-called \"Barbarians\" (Celts, Anglo-Saxons, Goths) in the 4th to 10th centuries. Graduate-level requirements include an in-depth research paper on a single aspect of current scholarly interest.
