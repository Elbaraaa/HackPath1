ARH512D - Medieval Art: Romanesque and Gothic
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008165
Course Description
This course examines Romanesque and Gothic art and architecture of the 12th to 14th centuries through the historical-political situation that gave rise to both urban and rural developments. Graduate-level requirements include an in-depth research paper on a single aspect of current scholarly interest.
