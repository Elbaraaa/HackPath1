ARH516B - Eighteenth Century Art II: France and Germany
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Course will examine the art and architecture of 18th-century France and Germany. Graduate-level requirements include supplemental reading, discussion; additional writing assignments and oral presentations.
