ARH523B - The Art of Mexico
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008187
Course Description
The art of Modern Mexico, from the late 18th century to the early 20th century. The Independence Period, the National Period, and the Revolutionary Period. Painting, sculpture, architecture, graphic and minor arts. Graduate-level requirements include a critical bibliography as well as a research paper.
