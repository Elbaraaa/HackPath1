ARH524A - History of Photography
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008188
Course Description
From its invention to 1895; impact of photography on the art and culture of the 19th century. Graduate-level requirements include an in-depth research paper on a single aspect of current scholarly interest.

Course Requisites
ARTMFA, ARHMA, AHEDPHD subplan AHS, MSTCRTG Graduates need priority to register for this course. Other graduates can register for this course by contacting the department.
