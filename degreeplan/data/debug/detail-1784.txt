ARH524C - History of Photography: Contemporary Trends
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008190
Course Description
This course surveys the role of photography in contemporary art. Themes include: the 'photo boom' of the 1970s, photography's relationship to other art movements, the rise of alternative and digital processes, and globalism. Graduate-level requirements include a longer paper with a methodological or historiographic statement and a presentation on the paper.
