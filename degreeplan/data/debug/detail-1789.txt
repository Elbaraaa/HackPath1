ARH531 - Studio Introduction to Contemporary Art
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008197
Course Description
Introduction to contemporary art theory, criticism, and cultural politics relevant to the study and practice of contemporary Art and Art History. Emphasis on methods, movements and themes. Graduate students will lead discussions, develop critical bibliography, produce reading summaries, and deliver a final project report.
