ARH596O - Issues in Art History
Art History  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008231
Course Description
Please note: the content of this course will change with the instructor at ASU. This course accommodates the video broadcast of established Art History courses at other universities. This is intended as an omnibus number so that students at the UA may register for a particular course. An example syllabus will be kept on file in the Art Dept. Graduate-level requirements include more extensive research paper as well as an oral presentation using primary sources.
