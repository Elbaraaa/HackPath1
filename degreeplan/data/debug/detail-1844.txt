ARL580 - Medicinal Plants
Plant Sciences, Sch  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
027338
Course Description
Historical and cultural aspects of plants and medicine, therapeutic uses of plants, psychoactive and food plants, contribution of medicinal plants to modern medicine, future of medicinal plants. Graduate-level requirements include review of at least two leading papers in the field.
