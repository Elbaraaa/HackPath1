ART101 - FYE: The Drawing Lab
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043453
Course Description
This introductory drawing course provides students with a comprehensive exploration of drawing techniques, emphasizing traditional methods, contemporary experimental practices, and figure drawing. Through a series of hands-on exercises, students will develop fundamental drawing skills while gaining an understanding of the historical context and contemporary applications of drawing as an artistic practice. Whether pursuing a career in fine arts, illustration, design, or simply seeking to enhance their artistic skills, this course provides a solid foundation for further exploration and development in the field of drawing.
