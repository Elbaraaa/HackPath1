ART200 - The Elements of Drawing
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041305
Course Description
The Elements of Drawing (Art 200) offers students a balanced and comprehensive curriculum of fundamental observational assignments. In Drawing 200, figure drawing is considered a prominent component of the discipline. Through a series of demonstrations and assignments, students will become proficient in both the technical and conceptual applications of numerous media including graphite, charcoal, conté crayon, pastels, and others at the instructor's discretion. Students will also be exposed to a wide variety of non-traditional materials, and will explore myriad ways of creating a \"work on paper\". Examples from the UAMA, drawing archive, faculty collections, and various texts will present the students with the work of historical and contemporary artists.
