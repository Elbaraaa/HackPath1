ART306B - Animation 1
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
033824
Course Description
An introduction to the practice, theory, and history of animation, this class will be a complete and immersive introduction to traditional 2D animation. It will focus on hand-made, lo-fi techniques that make it easier for one person to complete animated projects by themselves and develop a workflow that best serves their process. The focus of the class is to develop an understanding of the twelve principles of animation, and the underlying aspects that go into developing a successful animation.
