ART306C - Process and Play
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042803
Course Description
This is an intermediate level IDA course emphasizing exploration, play and failure as critical components of process in exploring methods of image creation and concept development. Utilizing both digital and analog tools, emphasis is placed on visual grammar, semiotics, color theory and the application of design principles in the service of creating meaning, problem solving and storytelling.
