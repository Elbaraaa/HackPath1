ART343B - Experimental Photographic Techniques
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008383
Course Description
Darkroom course exploring the contemporary use of 19th century photographic processes. Production includes one-of-a-kind prints with hand applied emulsions utilizing enlarged digital negatives. Processes may include: cyanotype, van dyke brown, salt, platinum/palladium, and gum bichromate.
