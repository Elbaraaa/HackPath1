ART346 - Imaging, Photography, Video and Imaging
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042478
Course Description
Students produce artwork at the intersections of photography and video with other digital media, including generative and immersive strategies. They investigate poetic possibilities and subject matter while being introduced to historical artwork, theory, and technological developments. Course explorations emphasize experimentation and critical application.
