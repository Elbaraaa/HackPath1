ART361 - UI/UX Design
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This course is an introduction to digital design focusing on front-end Web and App design, as well as UX (User Experience) and UI (User Interface). Students will be exposed to both the theory and current trends of designing and prototyping digitally delivered content.
