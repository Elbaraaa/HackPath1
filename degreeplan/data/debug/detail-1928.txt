ART363A - Typography I
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008401
Course Description
This course is an introduction to the core skills, elements, and principles of typography as well as the study of typographic forms for effective use in visual communication design. Students will learn about the typographic process, nomenclature, and compositional principles through typographic exercises and projects.
