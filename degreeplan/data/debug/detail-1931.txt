ART366 - Illustration Studio II
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
In this course students will continue to develop a visual language that can solve a communication problem, tell a story, create a fiction, relate a lived experience, evoke emotion, interpret text, visualize information and inspire contemplation in relation to an intended audience.
