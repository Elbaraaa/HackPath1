Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Materials and Methods in Painting is part one of the 380A/380B sequence. 380A is an intermediate level painting class in developing pictorial, technical and expressive skills in oil and/or acrylics. This class emphasizes a furthering of technical and formal issues in historical and contemporary painting and also includes concept and analysis of these issues.
