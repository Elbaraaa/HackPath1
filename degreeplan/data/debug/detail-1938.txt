General

Course Description
Contemporary Issues in Painting, (ART 380B), is part two of the 380A/380B sequence. 380B is an intermediate level painting class in developing concept and expressive and pictorial skills in oil and/or acrylics. This class emphasizes a furthering of contemporary approaches to content, media, format, technique, and composition.
