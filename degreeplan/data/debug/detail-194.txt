Course Description
A culminating experience for majors involving a substantive project and presentation that demonstrates a synthesis of learning accumulated in the major, including broadly comprehensive knowledge of the discipline. Graduate-level requirements include a project which includes business recommendations for a select audience based on the project research and analysis.
