ART389 - Intermediate 3D Figuration
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008430
Course Description
Intermediate techniques and concepts in clay modeling, mold making, casting in Forton, plastics, paper and other 3D processes that are based on the figure as a source. Scale, composition, gesture, surface and anatomical structure will be studied to develop creative solutions.
