ART426 - Alternative Process and Material: Collage and Construction
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
038960
Course Description
This Alternative Process and Materials course uses collage and construction as a means of conceptual development and source of innovative images and concepts. Methods for generating ideas for works will be used in the class. Some traditional methods and non traditional collage, assemblage and mixed media techniques will be presented. There will also be lecture, discussion and presentation periods on collage and assemblage movements, artists and concepts.
