ART446 - Advanced Imaging
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042548
Course Description
Advanced students produce artwork at the intersections of photography and video with other digital media, including generative, immersive, and interactive strategies. They investigate poetic possibilities and subject matter while being introduced to historical artwork, theory, and technological developments. Course explorations emphasize experimentation and critical application.
