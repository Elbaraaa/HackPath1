ART462B - Comics and Sequential Art
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008521
Course Description
This studio art class is designed to take students through the process of researching, creating, and producing work in the comic book genre for presentation within the comics industry. Advanced students will have the opportunity to learn techniques and approaches to an art medium that has thoroughly penetrated contemporary culture. Students will learn a variety of creative techniques for concept development for sequential art.
