Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Pictorial documentation of environments, characters and scenarios inspired by the Orvieto setting. Sketchbooks and travel journals will be used, story telling and visual essay techniques, handmade books, comics, graphic novels, picture books as objects of art explored.
