ART465 - Clients and the Community Design
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008525
Course Description
This is an advanced level IDA course which incorporates project-oriented class work with a focus on working with and for community-based clients. As we become more of a global community, clients may include regional, national and international organizations that focus on socially responsible practices and community building. Projects will integrate both print and digital modalities of design, illustration and animation. Filed trips, both on and off campus are a component of this class.
