ART497A - Gallery Praxis
Art  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008575
Course Description
Introduction to the essential aspects of gallery management: curating special events, installation, marketing, and grant writing. Introduction to various careers in the arts as well as professionalism and key business skills needed in the art world. Students have hands-on practical experience working in the Joseph Gross Gallery.
