ART532A - Interactivity
Art  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course explores the process of creating interactive computer art by teaching the essential principles of programming. Graduate-level requirements include a more in-depth research in related artworks and in-depth oral presentation of their findings. Secondly, they will organize the programming of the final collaborative project.
