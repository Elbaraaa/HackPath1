ART533A - Digital Arts in Context
Art  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008632
Course Description
This course examines the historical, cultural and technological aspects of digital arts from the mid 20th century to the present. It traces the evolution of the Art & Technology Movement to provide a historical framework. Graduate-level requirements include two in-depth oral presentations, a 6-8 page research paper and an 8-10 page final paper.
