ART562A - Figure Painting for Illustration
Art  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008661
Course Description
Study of the human form as a means of developing a personal aesthetic in painting/illustration. Graduate students will present a slide presentation/lecture about their own and three other artists' work. Give demonstration on color, delineation of form, use of materials and personal convictions to the class. Lead one workshop/critique.
