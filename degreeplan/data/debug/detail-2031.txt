ART562O - Libretto Concetti: Visual Narratives
Art  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008664
Course Description
Pictorial documentation of environments, characters and scenarios inspired by the Orvieto setting. Sketchbooks and travel journals will be used, story telling and visual essay techniques, handmade books, comics, graphic novels, picture books as objects of art explored. Graduate-level requirements include a research paper on a topic agreed upon with the instructor.
