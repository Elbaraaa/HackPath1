ART698 - Graduate Thesis Studio
Art  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008736
Course Description
Masters programs capstone or culmination experience that allows the student to prove the ability to synthesize material from course work and to apply information and knowledge to a specific issue or problem. The format of this research is a rigorous creative project and a culminating dissemination requirement, such as a public show or exhibition. Since masters programs also hone their students ability to communicate in ones field, the culminating requirement typically includes opportunity for the student to present and document a creative body of work. In order to accommodate these goals there is required an oral presentation or defense of the masters thesis or project as well as documentation of the creative research project.
