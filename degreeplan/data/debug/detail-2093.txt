ASM504 - Irrigation Principles and Management
Agricultural Education  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Principles of operating farm irrigation systems, evaluation of systems, selection of systems, basic irrigation scheduling, measurements of water flow, soil moisture, pump and system efficiencies. Graduate-level requirements include a special project on a current irrigation topic.
