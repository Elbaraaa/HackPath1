ASTR204 - Great Debates in Astronomy
Astronomy  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008809
Course Description
This course examines questions such as: How old is the Universe? Where did the Moon come from? Should astronauts or robots explore space? Students will study the methods and personalities behind key discoveries, participate in classroom debates, and argue their positions in detailed essays.
