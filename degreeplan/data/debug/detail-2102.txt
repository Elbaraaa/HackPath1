Astronomy  Undergraduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
This course will cover the methods, tools and perspectives to understand energy generation and use, focusing on traditional and alternative energy sources. The goal is to develop scientific and critical thinking in issues related to the technical and economic aspects, as well as policy decisions.
