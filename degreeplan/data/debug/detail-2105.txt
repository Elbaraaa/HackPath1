ASTR250 - Fundamentals of Astronomy
Astronomy  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008814
Course Description
A calculus-based introductory course in astronomy and astrophysics, aimed mainly at sophomore astronomy majors and science majors from other departments. The class covers most aspects of astronomy, including stars, galaxies, cosmology and the solar system, but with a more mathematical treatment than in the 100-level Natural Science classes.
