ASTR475 - Planetary Astrobiology
Astronomy  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
036537
Course Description
The course will explore the processes related to planet formation and the emergence of life. We will study the formation of our Solar System and exoplanetary systems, the conditions that gave rise to life on the Earth, and the potential habitability of other planets/moons in our system or extrasolar systems.
