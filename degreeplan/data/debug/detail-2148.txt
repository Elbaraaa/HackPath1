ASTR541 - Extragalactic Astronomy and Cosmology
Astronomy  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
008880
Course Description
The structure, origin and evolution of the physical universe from theory and observations of systems outside our own galaxy. Relativistic cosmology; galaxy evolution and clustering; active galaxies and quasars; the microwave background; galaxy formation; the hot big bang; and physics of the early universe.
