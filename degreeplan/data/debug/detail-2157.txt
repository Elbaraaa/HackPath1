ASTR582 - High-Energy Astrophysics
Astronomy  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
036457
Course Description
A study of pulsars, black holes, accretion disks, X-ray binaries, gamma-ray sources, radio galaxies, active galactic nuclei, and the acceleration of charged particles near these objects, together with the radiation mechanisms they employ to produce the high-energy emission we detect at Earth.
