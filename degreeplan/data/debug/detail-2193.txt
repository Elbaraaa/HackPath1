ATMO474B - Weather Analysis and Forecasting II
Hydrology & Atmospheric Sci  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040203
Course Description
This course is for senior undergraduate and graduate students. The overall goal of this course is to apply the fundamental theoretical principles of synoptic-dynamic and mesoscale meteorology to the real atmosphere through a discussion of ensemble weather forecasting, an application of quasi-geostrophic principles and potential vorticity thinking to weather and forecasting, an overview of the dynamics of convective storms, and a real-time severe weather and quantitative precipitation forecasting exercise.
