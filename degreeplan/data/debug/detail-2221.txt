ATMO571 - Synoptic Meteorology
Hydrology & Atmospheric Sci  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009000
Course Description
Principles of meteorological analysis; fundamental concepts of dynamic meteorology. Structure and dynamics of mid-latitude cyclones and fronts. Use of computer driven graphical displays. Graduate-level requirements include a more quantitative and thorough understanding of the subject matter.
