ACCT430 - Information Quality and Assurance - A Management Perspective
Accountancy, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005838
Course Description
This class is centered on lecture and discussion of professional standards, ethical foundations of the accounting profession, landmark fraud cases, and extensive readings. Emphasis is on information quality, in regards to assurance services and internal control systems. The material discussed in this course often encompasses gray areas requiring a thoughtful approach to finding the best answer to questions that may not be explicitly asked and sometimes related to multiple, less-than-optimal, possibilities.
