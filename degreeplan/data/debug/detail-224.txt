ACCT451 - Analysis of Financial Statements
Accountancy, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005841
Course Description
This capstone course consists of three parts. In the first part, students seek to understand the company and its business, and to measure profitability and credit risk. In the second part, students will explore issues related to the income statement and balance sheet. The third part is focused on developing tools for forecasting pro forma financial statements, and considering basic issues related to company valuation and investment potential. Topics covered include structured analysis of financial statements, forecasting of income and cash flows, pro-forma financial statements, firm valuation using discounted cash flows and discounted residual income methods, comparative valuation analysis, and credit analysis.
