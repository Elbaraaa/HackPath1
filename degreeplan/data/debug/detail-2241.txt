BASV201 - Introduction to Global Security
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043570
Course Description
This course provides an introduction to global security, addressing traditional challenges such as warfare and national defense, alongside emerging threats like cyber-attacks and climate change. It explores critical topics, including the causes of international conflict, the role of non-state actors, military alliances, human security, and responses to terrorism and pandemics. The course combines theoretical analysis with historical examples to illustrate the evolution of global security. Students will develop analytical tools to critically assess contemporary and future security challenges.
