BASV405 - Advanced Human Factors
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043161
Course Description
Advanced Human Factors offers a comprehensive exploration of designing, evaluating, and understanding human-machine systems. Gain proficiency in applying concepts, effective communication, and innovative design methodologies. This course fosters a holistic grasp of Human Factors (HF) and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) while delving into pivotal subjects like design thinking, ethics, critical thinking, and forward-thinking problem-solving. Through the examination of advanced technologies encompassing AI, VR/AR/XR, brain augmentation, space, cyber, ethical considerations, and societal impacts, you'll be primed for a career in the thriving realm of human factors and human-computer interaction.
