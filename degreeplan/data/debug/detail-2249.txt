BASV498A - Human Factors Capstone Senior I Course
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043187
Course Description
BASV 498A is first semester of the culminating experience of your undergraduate experience in the human factors program. In this course, you will focus on applied research to equip you with the skills and knowledge needed for a successful career in cybersecurity, defense, and other domains. This experience offers a unique opportunity to address practical challenges, develop innovative solutions, and gain hands-on experience in integrating theory, experimental testing, analytical insight, and systematic (and creative!) design. This course is the first part of senior capstone process that will set the groundwork and provide the necessary details to complete you final Human Factors project.
