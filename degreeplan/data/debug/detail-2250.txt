BASV498B - Human Factors Capstone Senior II Course
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043188
Course Description
BASV 498B is the second semester of the culminating experience of your undergraduate experience in the human factors program. In this course, you will complete you applied research project and learn the skills and knowledge needed for a successful career in cybersecurity, defense, and other domains. Through your capstone work, you'll not only showcase your research competence and deep understanding of these fields but also enhance your career readiness, positioning you for a smooth transition into your career in the field of Human Factors. This course is the last part of the senior capstone process that will help transitioning graduate school or the Human Factors field.
