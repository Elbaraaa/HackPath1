BAT396 - Intermediate Special Topics in Biosystems Analytics & Technology
Biosystems Engineering  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042069
Course Description
This course provides an exploration of varying special topics within the field of biosystems analytics and technology. The topics of the course vary depending on instructor and semester. Each course will overview core concepts, foundational knowledge, and practical applications about the topics presented. The course will focus on contemporary issues in the field and those issues' contextual positioning within human health and environmental contexts.
