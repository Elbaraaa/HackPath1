BAT496A - Biosystems Careers and Professionalism Seminar
Biosystems Engineering  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005710
Course Description
The seminar focuses on honing career development skills introduced in BAT/BE 201 for employment in the biosystems engineering and analytics fields and becoming exposed to ethics and professionalism. Topics include how to find a job (finding opportunities, writing elements for the interviewing process, interviewing, networking), the value of continuing education (professional societies, schools, self-learning), and ethics. Presentations, discussions, and guest speakers provide communication opportunities.

Engineering Students enrolled in BE 496A are required to register and take the Fundamentals of Engineering (FE) Exam.
