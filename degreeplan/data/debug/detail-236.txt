Accountancy, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course covers the examination of the value of managerial accounting in organizational decision-making and control, addressing specific managerial accounting problems and their solutions. The general goal of this course is to enhance your conceptual and applied knowledge of management accounting, especially with respect to recent developments in the field.
