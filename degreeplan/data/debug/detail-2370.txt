BE559 - Design of Onsite Wastewater Treatment and Dispersal Systems
Biosystems Engineering  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course will cover issues and concepts relating to the design of domestic and small commercial onsite wastewater treatment and recycling systems. Graduate-level requirements include an additional design project in order to receive graduate credit. This course is typically offered every even spring semester.
