BE592 - Directed Research
Biosystems Engineering  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040870
Course Description
Graduate student will participate a faculty-led research within the University of Arizona as an individual or as a small group. The faculty member will provide clear objectives at the beginning of the class, and meet with the student on a regular basis to track his/her progress. Towards the end of the class, the student needs to make oral presentation(s) in laboratory meeting and submit a written report to the faculty member. Department Consent is required.
