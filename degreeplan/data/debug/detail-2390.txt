Biosystems Engineering  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Dedicated to the exchange of scholarly information, usually in a small group setting. The scope of work shall consist of participation in seminar by course registrants, with the exchange of ideas presented in such seminars through discussion. Registrants will also preside over one or more seminars and otherwise provide support to the speakers and attendees.
