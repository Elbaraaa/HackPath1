Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
An introduction to the ethical issues pertaining to the biological research field and current bioethical concerns in medicine, discussions of bioethical concerns in society, and practical experience in scientific oral presentations and professional writing.
