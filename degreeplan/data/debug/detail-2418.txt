BIOC434 - Biology Teaching Methods for Secondary Teachers
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009178
Course Description
Methods for students preparing to teach secondary science. Students will create curriculum and lesson plans, compile resources for science teaching, examine lab safety regulations, and practice teaching science lessons. Assumes knowledge of reform-based teaching theory.
