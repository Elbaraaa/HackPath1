BIOC466 - Biochemistry of Nucleic Acids
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009192
Course Description
The biochemistry of nucleic acids including replication, repair, recombination, restriction of DNA, transcription, processing and translation of RNA, gene regulation and biochemical and genomic techniques to study these processes with a molecular emphasis. Designed primarily for majors and minors in biochemistry and chemistry.
