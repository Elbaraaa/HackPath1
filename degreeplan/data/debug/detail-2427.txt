BIOC472 - Advanced Genetics for Teachers
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
036803
Course Description
Principles of genetics with a focus on applications and current research appropriate for teachers of secondary school biology. This course is designed for prospective and in-service science teachers who wish to develop a deeper understanding of central ideas in genetics. Core topics include the molecular basis for inheritance, basic descriptive genetics and the mathematical patterns that explain genetic patterns, gene regulation, genomics, and the use of genetic technologies to address human problems. This course is designed to be on-line. In-service science teachers may take the course for graduate credit by completing additional graduate-level course work.
