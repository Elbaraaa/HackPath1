BIOC585A - Biological Structure 1
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039181
Course Description
Biological Structure I covers one of two major methods for acquiring atomic structures of proteins and nucleic acids, X-ray crystallography. Biological Structure II covers the other major method, NMR spectroscopy. The emphasis is more conceptual than mathematical and focuses on the strengths and weaknesses of each approach.
