Chemistry and Biochemistry  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Microbiology for Teachers is a graduate-level online course for secondary school teachers. Teachers enrolled in the course will refresh their knowledge of microbiology, discuss current advances in the field, and develop ways to incorporate microbiology into their curricula.
