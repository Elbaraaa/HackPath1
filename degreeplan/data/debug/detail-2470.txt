BIOS216 - Understanding Our Health Through Data
Epidemiology and Biostatistics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043046
Course Description
How can collecting data from our personal lives help us better understand our health? Can the tools of a natural scientist inform our decisions about how we manage our own health? In this class students will work together to understand and define problems regarding their health, plan what to measure and how, collect and manage data, visualize and analyze that data, and come to their own interpretations and conclusions.
