BIOS451 - Health Data Management and Visualization
Epidemiology and Biostatistics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This course will teach students basic data management principles as well as techniques for data management and visualization. Students will develop hands-on skills of managing research data and using visualization techniques in population health studies and clinical investigations.
