Community, Environment & Pol  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
The fundamental principles of public health are presented for students in any graduate academic program. The course explores theoretical aspects of public health and provides practical application designed to improve understanding and performance of researchers in public health settings. Mastery of course objectives will provide academic graduate students with a clear working knowledge of public health basics.
