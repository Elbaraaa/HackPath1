Health Promotional Services  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Concepts for the critical analysis of the scientific literature within public health and the broader field of medicine are reviewed. Topics including understanding the research stream, study design, statistical methodology, and the accurate presentation of results are addressed.
