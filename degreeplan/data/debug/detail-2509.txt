BIOS682 - Introduction to Genomic Data Science
Epidemiology and Biostatistics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042867
Course Description
This course will teach students basic concepts of probability as well as statistical methods for next-generation sequencing (NGS) data, including single-cell and spatial transcriptomics data. Students will use R and Python to perform statistical analyses on biological datasets.
