BIOS687 - Theory of Linear Models
Epidemiology and Biostatistics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
033838
Course Description
Theory of linear models including full-rank models and less than full rank fixed effects models. Topics will include distributional properties of quadratic forms, estimation methods, tests of hypothesis and confidence intervals as well as an introduction to computational aspects.
