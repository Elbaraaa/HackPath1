Biomedical Engineering  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course covers application of statistics to biomedical engineering and research. Topics include describing and summarizing biomedical data, study designs, probability distributions, diagnostic testing, and statistical inference for biomedical applications. All topics will involve use of R Statistical Computing Software.
