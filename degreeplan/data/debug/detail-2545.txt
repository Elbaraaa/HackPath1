Biosystems Engineering  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Principles of electric circuits towards sensor and biosensor design and fabrication. Selection, interfacing and calibration of digital and analog sensors to measure physical, chemical, and biological variables. Optical and electrochemical biosensors. Advanced biosensors.
