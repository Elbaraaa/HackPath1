BME485 - Nanoscience and Nanotechnology for Biomedical Engineers
Biomedical Engineering  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
038510
Course Description
This course explores the role of nanomaterials in biomedical engineering applications, focusing on how nanoscale technologies interact with and enhance biological systems. Students will examine topics such as synthetic biology, drug delivery, and bioelectronics, gaining insight into how these technologies support diagnostics, therapeutics, and tissue engineering. Emphasis is placed on understanding the fundamental principles and unique properties of nanomaterials, as well as translating cutting-edge research into real-world medical solutions. Designed for upper-level undergraduates and entry-level graduate students.
