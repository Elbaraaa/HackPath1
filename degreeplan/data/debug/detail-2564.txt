BME516 - Biomedical Imaging
Biomedical Engineering  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009384
Course Description
Introduction to the mathematical, physical, engineering and biological principles important to a variety of biomedical imaging methods including optical imaging, x-ray imaging, CT, PET, SPECT and MRI. Graduate-level requirements include a written term paper and an oral presentation. See syllabus for complete course requirements and grading scale.
