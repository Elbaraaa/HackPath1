ACCT557A - Partnership Taxation
Accountancy, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course focuses on federal tax laws primarily related to partnership taxation. However, coverage will also include estate, gift, and trust taxation. In addition to covering the fundamental concepts related to these areas of taxation, a primary focus of the course will be on learning to prepare tax returns using appropriate tax software.
