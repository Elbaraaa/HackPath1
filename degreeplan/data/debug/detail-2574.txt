BME583 - Micro Biomechanics
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005538
Course Description
Thermodynamics, mechanics, and structures of biomolecules (e.g., proteins and DNA) and cells. Deformation mechanisms and theories for both flexible and semi-rigid chains, and the applications in biomolecules and cells. Experimental micro biomechanics techniques for both biomolecules and cells. Graduate-level requirements include comprehensively review one of the mainstream experimental techniques in micro biomechanics and submit a final report.
