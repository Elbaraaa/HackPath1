BME588 - Bioprinting of Functional Tissues
Biomedical Engineering  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043633
Course Description
This course introduces the foundation, knowledge, skills and applications of tissue engineering and 3D bioprinting of functional tissues. A series of lectures and hands-on experimental sessions will be offered in cell culture basics, cellular imaging and analysis, organoid formation, and advanced bioprinting.
