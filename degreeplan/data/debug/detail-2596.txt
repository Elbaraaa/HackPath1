Eller Administration  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course is designed to assist students to develop the strategies, skills, confidence, and organization necessary to be a successful student at the collegiate level and as a business-intended student. Topics discussed include current academic challenges, learning strategies, time management, communication skills, and problem solving.
