Eller Administration  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This is one of three upper division courses that is required to complete a business minor in the Eller College of Management under the Business Administration category. This is an applications based course which looks at the financial and economic operations of domestic and world markets. This course can not be used towards any business major degree.
