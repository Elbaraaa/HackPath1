ACCT562D - Accounting Data Analytics
Accountancy, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course will introduce you to data analytics, with a focus on identifying and interpreting financial relationships using data analytic software. This will be accomplished by completing a multi-level case study in data analytics software, analyzing and responding to case study questions, and preparing a final cumulative report.
