Eller Administration  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Success requires much more than expertise in your field. Leadership in the 21st century requires developing a vision and driving your organization to excel through clear strategic goal-setting and execution. This module provides an integrated leadership framework for organizational success, combining leadership and team building strategies to provide the participants with actionable ideas they can implement immediately.
