BNAD505 - Strategic Innovation
Eller Administration  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009453
Course Description
This module will examine the innovation process including the development of the business case necessary for achieving success in the market. In the course of the module students either individually or in teams will be expected to define the scope, timelines and milestones for completing their project during the remaining modules of the program. In addition, they will apply the learning from this module to plan a product or service introduction from their assigned company.
