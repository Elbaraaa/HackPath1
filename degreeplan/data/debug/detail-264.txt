ACCT575 - Financial and Managerial Accounting in Healthcare
Accountancy, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040514
Course Description
This course covers financial and managerial accounting topics relevant to the healthcare industry. Concepts covered include the financial and operational implications of healthcare reimbursement methodologies; understanding and analyzing external financial statements for taxable and tax-exempt healthcare entities; usefulness of capital and operating budgets; practical applications of managerial accounting; and decision-making tools for managers within healthcare enterprises.
