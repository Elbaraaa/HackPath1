BNAD566 - Health Care Technology
Eller Administration  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039382
Course Description
This IT survey course will provide the MBA candidate with an overview of the technology issues and challenges within health care. Inclusive will be discussions around electronic medical records, the impacts around CMS policies and regulations on the health care technology environment, understanding the impacts of security, compliance, privacy and cybersecurity, the `Internet of things' as it is related to medical devices in the caregiving environment, as well as the significant challenges in managing large quantities of data. The course will also be `modern' and provide extra emphasis on the consumerization of health care as well as a bigger focus on ambulatory clinical care and wellness.
