BNAD596E - Global Business Perspectives
Eller Administration  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009481
Course Description
This course will provide students exposure on some of the economic, political, competitive and financial conditions impacting national and industrial sector performance in an increasingly global and volatile marketplace. The course will illustrate business concepts using an experiential model in which students travel to a particular region of the world for a 10-day trip.
