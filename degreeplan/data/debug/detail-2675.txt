Business Analytics, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043388
Course Description
Enterprise Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a subset of AI focused on driving business value in large-scale organizations. This course provides an overview and improved understanding of enterprise artificial intelligence in the context of its impact on business transformation. The course is divided into three main sections that will provide a historical overview of Artificial Intelligence (AI) with an emphasis on data warehousing of big data and an overview of the ethical framework for AI, applications of AI in transforming business applications across the industrial spectrum, and how businesses can employ AI to gain a competitive advantage while considering future governance.
