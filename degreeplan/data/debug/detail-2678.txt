BSM100X - BS in Medicine Major Preparation
Medicine, College of, Tucson  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042729
Course Description
Bachelor of Science in Medicine major students will learn about the College of Medicine's programs and services; have the opportunity to meet faculty members; learn about resources and services at the UA that promote retention; develop a deeper understanding of medical professions; gain experience in college success strategies; develop a graduation plan; and learn about the career opportunities after graduation.
