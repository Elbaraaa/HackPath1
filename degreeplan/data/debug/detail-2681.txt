BSM296 - Careers in Medical Health Sciences
Medicine, College of, Tucson  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041963
Course Description
This is an introductory Core course in the BS in Medicine concentration. It will provide students an opportunity to gain insight into how medicine applies to various disciplines involved in medicine and health sciences. These will include Medicine, Nursing, Public Health, Pharmacy, Social Work, Psychology, Nutrition, Occupational/Physical Therapy and Law. Through an interactive format, students will be challenged with various patient cases to consider the role that each of these disciplines plays in the care of the patient.
