Medicine, College of, Tucson  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042027
Course Description
This course will explore the foundations of wellbeing, promoters of wellbeing, detractors from wellbeing, and the systemic and organizational issues that are unique to the healthcare system. Students will learn and practice strategies to build healthy resilience, manage chronic stress, prevent burnout, and practice mindfulness. This Healthcare Professional Wellbeing Course includes concepts and curriculum appropriate for learners interested in any health care career. There are three components of the course: online content (asynchronous), wellness behaviors practices and reflections (individual and asynchronous), weekly in person/zoom class (synchronous and mandatory attendance).
