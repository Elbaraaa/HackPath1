BSM315 - Women, Health, and Society
Medicine, College of, Tucson  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043479
Course Description
This course examines the intersection of women, healthcare, and social determinants of health. Students will explore topics related to women's health, career trajectories, implicit bias, and advocacy. Through lectures, discussions, and case studies, students will gain insights into the challenges faced by women in healthcare and will discuss strategies to promote equity.
