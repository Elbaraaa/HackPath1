Course Description
This course examines the historical and contemporary influences on reproductive healthcare in Western societies, focusing on the United States. Students will explore how societal perceptions, advancing medical knowledge, clinical practices, and health policies have historically shaped and continue to influence the provision of reproductive healthcare services, and in turn, gender-based health outcomes.
