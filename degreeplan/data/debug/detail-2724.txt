Course Description
CALS 297B is an additional class students in the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences Arizona's Science, Engineering, and Math Scholars (CALS ASEMS) program can take to further develop their career preparation and research exploration skills related to their pursuit of STEM degrees and careers. The purpose of the CALS ASEMS program is to support CALS STEM students in successfully completing STEM degrees with a strong GPA.
