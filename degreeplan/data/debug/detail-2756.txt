Cancer Biology, GIDP  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
University faculty and national and international invited speakers present cancer-related research seminars for this series. This seminar series is attended by basic and clinical faculty, postdoctoral fellows, graduate students and research staff. The seminars are also broadcast to the Phoenix UA College of Medicine, the Translational Genomics Research Institute (TGEN) and the Arizona Cancer Center/Greater Phoenix Campus.
