Accountancy, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
The objectives of this course are to: 1) Provide an overview of the major areas of empirical accounting research, 2) Develop the ability to critically evaluate empirical accounting research, and 3) Develop the skills needed to both identify compelling research topics and execute a sound research design.
