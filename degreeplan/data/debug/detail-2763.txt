CBIO695C - Readings in Cancer Immunology
Immunobiology  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course will focus on the analysis and discussion of current basic and clinical immunological studies of cancer in the literature and on the search for ways to control the disease. Immunological surveillance plays an important role in cancer. Dysregulation of the immune system contributes to the poorer outcome in the disease.
