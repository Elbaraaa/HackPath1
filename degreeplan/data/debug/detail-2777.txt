CE281 - Drones in Engineering & Construction
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042269
Course Description
Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAVs) or drones have a variety of applications in engineering and construction. In this course you will learn about drone deployment for surveying, mapping, quantity takeoff, progress updates, business development, thermal imaging, and inspection. Drones can be used to facilitate all project phases including feasibility, design, bidding, preconstruction, construction, handover, and maintenance. This course provides preparation for part 107 FAA certification for drone pilots.
