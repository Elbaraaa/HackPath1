Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
Statistical decision theory and its application in civil engineering. Identification and modeling of non-deterministic problems in civil engineering and the treatment thereof relative to engineering design and decision making. Statistical reliability concepts.
