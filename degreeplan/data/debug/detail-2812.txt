CE433 - Advanced Structural Analysis
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009562
Course Description
[Usually offered every other Spring beginning 2002] Analysis of Structures: beams, frames and trusses. Statically indeterminate structures with stiffness methods: slope-deflection and moment distribution. Energy methods. Matrix structural analysis and computer applications. Qualitative influence lines and approximate solutions of statically indeterminate structures.
