Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
Introduction to geoenvironmental engineering; physiochemical and microstructural behavior of geomaterials, effect of pollutants, design of waste disposal systems; advanced laboratory testing, geotextiles, space geomechanics, etc. Usually offered every third semester starting Spring 2003.
