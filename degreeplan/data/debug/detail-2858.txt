CE523 - Hydrology
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009624
Course Description
Discussion and analysis of major topics of the hydrologic cycle and their interrelationship, such as rainfall, infiltration, evaporation, and runoff. Statistical and probabilistic methods in water supply and flood hydrology. Graduate-level requirements include a project paper.
