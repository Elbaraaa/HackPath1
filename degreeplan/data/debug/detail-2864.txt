CE531 - Sustainable Materials Design Lab
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042598
Course Description
This class aims to understand the nano-structural to macrostructure behavior of materials used in the world to design for low-CO2 and sustainable materials. This class will be structured into two modules designed to help students understand the material's chemo-mechanical characteristics with multiscale characterization methods and designing sustainable and low-CO2 materials.
