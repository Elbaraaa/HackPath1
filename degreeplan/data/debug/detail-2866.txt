CE534 - Design of Wood and Masonry Structures
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009633
Course Description
[Usually offered every third semester beginning Spring 2002] Determination of gravity and lateral loads on structures. Design of wood structures for axial load and bending; structural wood panels, diaphragms and shear walls. Types of masonry construction. Graduate-level requirements include a comprehensive design project.
