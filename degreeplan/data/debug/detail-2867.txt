CE535 - Prestressed Concrete Structures
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009634
Course Description
Behavior, analysis and design of statically determinate and indeterminate prestressed concrete structures; calculation of loss of prestress. Usually offered every other Spring beginning 2003. Graduate-level requirements may include a research paper or a comprehensive design project.
