CE543 - Block Theory Applications for Rock Mass Stability
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
035227
Course Description
The objective of the short course is to show the applications of block theory for rock mass surficial and underground excavations. Three papers will be given to illustrate the application of theory to shiplock slopes of the Three Gorges dam site, China, a mine in Arizona and a highway rock slope in Arizona.
