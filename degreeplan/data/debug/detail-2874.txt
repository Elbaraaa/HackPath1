CE544 - Special Topics In Geomechanics
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009641
Course Description
Introduction to geoenvironmental engineering; physiochemical and microstructural behavior of geomaterials, effect of pollutants, design of waste disposal systems; advanced laboratory testing, geotextiles, space geomechanics, etc. Usually offered every third semester starting Spring 2003. Graduate-level requirements include a research paper and/or a comprehensive design project.
