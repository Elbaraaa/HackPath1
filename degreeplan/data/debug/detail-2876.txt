CE546 - Geotechnical Earthquake Engineering
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009643
Course Description
Review of plate tectonics and seismology, analysis of earthquake ground motions, travel path and distance effects, and site response effects. Soil liquefaction susceptibility, identification, and mitigation. Introduction to seismic slope stability. Graduate level requirements include a research paper and/or a comprehensive design project.
