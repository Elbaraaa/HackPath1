CE548 - Numerical Methods in Geotechnical Engineering
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009645
Course Description
Brief statements and applications of numerical methods based on closed-form solutions, finite difference and finite element methods for problems involving soil structure interaction such as piles, retaining walls, group piles, underground works; seepage; and consolidation. Graduate-level requirements include additional assignments, e.g. a research paper on specific topics.
