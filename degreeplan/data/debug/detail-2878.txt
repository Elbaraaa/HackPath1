Hydrology & Atmospheric Sci  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Application of statistics and probability to uncertainty in the description, measurement, and analysis of hydrologic variables and processes, including extreme events, error models, simulation, sampling. Graduate-level requirements include an in-depth simulation project.
