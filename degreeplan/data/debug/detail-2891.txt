CE567 - Traffic Safety
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040060
Course Description
The following course is intended to introduce topics in traffic safety. Included will be information on how to understand and utilize crash data, safety analysis methods described in the Highway Safety Manual, statistical methods in safety analysis, human factors and crash causality, and an overview of other emerging safety issues and resources.
