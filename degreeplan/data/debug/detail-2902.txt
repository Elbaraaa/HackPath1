CE585 - Construction Equipment and Methods
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039409
Course Description
Develop an enhanced understanding of construction equipment and methods to contribute to construction firms, project management consultants, and owners upon graduation. Topics include: costing, safety, earth moving equipment, cranes, creating and securing deep digs, constructing deep foundations, and forms and temporary structures. Graduate students will be expected to complete additional or alternate problems throughout the semester and present content and problem solutions in class.
