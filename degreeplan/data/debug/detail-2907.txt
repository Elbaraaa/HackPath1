CE597 - Special Topics in Construction
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042212
Course Description
Construction engineering management draws on knowledge from many areas including engineering, technology, and management. Course content and scope of work will vary depending on the selected topic. This course will emphasize selected topics covering the life span of construction projects from pre-construction, to construction, closeout and decommissioning. This course will draw from multiple industry sectors including heavy, industrial, commercial, and mixed use construction.
