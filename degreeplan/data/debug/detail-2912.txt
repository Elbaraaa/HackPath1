CE633 - Reinforced Concrete
Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009699
Course Description
[Usually offered every other Fall beginning 2002] Inelastic behavior of beams and columns; short- and long-term beam deflections; combined bending, shear, and torsion in beams; behavior under load reversals; analysis and design of beam to column connections and shear walls.
