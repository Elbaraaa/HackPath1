CHEE295E - Careers in Environmental Engineering
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
038955
Course Description
The main course objective is to familiarize students with the possible careers in the environmental engineering field. The course is provided in colloquium style and designed to help students understand career opportunities for Environmental Engineers (EEN). Students will interact with invited speakers and explore various roles of EEN in solving real environmental engineering problems.
