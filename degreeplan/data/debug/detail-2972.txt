CHEE331 - Heat and Mass Transfer for Environmental Engineers
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043384
Course Description
The course will introduce the basic concepts governing heat and mass transfer in natural and engineered systems and develop the general equations that describe these processes. The applications will emphasize processes in environmental science and engineering.
