CHEE375 - Environmental and Water Engineering
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043628
Course Description
Application of theory and engineering experience to design of unit operations for production of potable water. Covers reactor design and kinetics, conventional water treatment technologies and selected advance treatment topics.
