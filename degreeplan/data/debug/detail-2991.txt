CHEE416 - Electrochemistry for Energy and Sustainability
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043620
Course Description
This course introduces the principles of electrochemistry, focusing on thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, and transport phenomena, with specific emphasis on their role in energy and sustainability technologies. Students will gain a deep understanding of the principles governing electrochemical systems and their potential to address global energy and environmental challenges.
