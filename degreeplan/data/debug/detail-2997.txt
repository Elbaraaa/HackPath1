CHEE438 - Intermolecular and Surface Forces
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Development of quantitative understanding of the different intermolecular and surface forces between molecules and how these forces give rise to interesting phenomena at the nano and microscale.
