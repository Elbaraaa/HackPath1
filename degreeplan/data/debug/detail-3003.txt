CHEE477R - Microbiology for Engineers
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
010410
Course Description
This course focuses on the principles of microbiology, including physiology, metabolism, genetics and ecology. The course explores fundamental microbial processes as well as their environmental significance and application in environmental engineering. A laboratory is associated with the lecture course to provide laboratory skills in basic and applied microbiology.
