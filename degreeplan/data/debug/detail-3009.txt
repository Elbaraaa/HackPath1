CHEE485 - Water Reuse
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042315
Course Description
The water reuse course is a senior undergrad/graduate student engineering elective course designed for students interested in learning the regulatory framework, the microbiological and chemical contaminants of concern, and the treatment technologies utilized for potable water reuse. Treatment options will be analyzed and several case studies will be examined. The course includes lectures, discussion sessions, and software simulations.
