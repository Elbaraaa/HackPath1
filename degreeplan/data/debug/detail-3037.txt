CHEE572 - Interfacial Chemistry of Biomolecules in Environmental Systems
Environmental Science  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
032373
Course Description
Taught in even years. Introduction to the chemical and adhesive properties of macromolecules at interfaces. The fundamental physico-chemical forces that govern adsorption of macromolecules at interfaces and inter-particle adhesion will be discussed. Topics such as surface tension, self-assembly, adsorption of polymers and biomolecules, and bacterial cell adhesion will be discussed with emphasis on environmental applications.
