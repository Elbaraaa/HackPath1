CHEE587 - Topics in Transport Phenomena
Chemical & Environmental Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039402
Course Description
Special topics in transport phenomena oriented towards practical applications in specific industrial and research areas. Topics include the dynamics of non-Newtonian fluids, thermal radiation, transport in multiphases systems, design of fluid transport systems, atmospheric transport, and mechanics of interfaces. Graduate level requirements include a special term project.
