Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
CHEM 143 is the first semester of a two-semester laboratory sequence designed to provide an introduction to the central principles and practices of modern quantitative chemical analysis.

Enrollment Requirements
