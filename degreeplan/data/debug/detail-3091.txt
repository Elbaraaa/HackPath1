CHEM227 - Foundations of Analytical Chemistry
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043505
Course Description
This course focuses on the foundations of modern analytical chemistry, especially the chemistry, measurement principles, and quantitative methods involved. It covers atomic analytical methods, including electrochemistry and spectroscopy, as well as molecular analysis via separations and mass spectrometry. Statistics and chemical equilibria will be covered throughout the course as will applied computer programming and data science methods.
