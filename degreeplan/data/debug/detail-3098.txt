CHEM244A - Honors Organic Chemistry Laboratory
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
010533
Course Description
An introduction to the organic chemistry laboratory with an emphasis on development of laboratory skills and techniques, observation of chemical phenomena, data collection, and the interpretation and reporting of results in formal laboratory reports. Heavy emphasis on microscale techniques, laboratory safety, waste disposal and biochemical applications.
Min Units

Enrollment Requirements
Student must be active in the Honors College. CHEM 142/144 or CHEM 152 or CHEM 162/164 or CHEM 182, completion or concurrent enrollment in CHEM 241A, CHEM 242A or CHEM 246A.
Course Requisites
Credit Allowed for only one of the following, CHEM 243A or CHEM 244A or CHEM 247A.
