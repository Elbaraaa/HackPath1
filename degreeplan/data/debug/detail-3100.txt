CHEM246 - Principles Of Organic Chemistry
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043563
Course Description
This is a one-semester course designed to introduce students to the foundations and central principles of organic chemistry, including the use/interpretation of line-drawn structures, names of functional groups, 3D attributes of molecules, general reactivity, and electron arrow pushing mechanisms.
