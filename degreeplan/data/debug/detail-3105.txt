CHEM256L - Synthesis Laboratory
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043564
Course Description
This course provides an introduction to the methods that are commonly encountered in the handling and analysis of chemical materials. Students gain experience using equipment and techniques for carrying out a range of reactions, separations, and purifications. Methods for the analysis and characterization of organic compounds are also investigated. The skills gained in these courses are broadly applicable to many different areas of science involving the handling of organic compounds.
