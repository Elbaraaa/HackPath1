CHEM257A - The Organic Chemistry and Materials Science of Art and Archaeological Objects
Materials Science & Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
022865
Course Description
We explore the roles of organic chemistry and materials science in the formulation, ageing and conservation of art and archaeological objects. Topics include color and bonding theories, and the organic molecules in paints, paper, gels, dyes, textiles, adhesives and polymers.
