CHEM295A - Second Year Chemistry Colloquium
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This one-unit course focuses on core skills and topics essential for success in the chemistry profession, including navigating scientific literature, safety, ethics, communication, record keeping, data presentation, and scientific presentations. We will explore research opportunities and career options and how to select coursework to best prepare for particular career trajectories.
