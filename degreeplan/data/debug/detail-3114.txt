CHEM310 - Principles of Inorganic Chemistry
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043815
Course Description
This course provides an introduction to the field of modern Inorganic Chemistry with a focus on conveying its impact in a range of areas: from materials and technology to catalysis, from the role of metals in biology to metal-based therapeutics.
