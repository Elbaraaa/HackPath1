CHEM346 - Advanced Principles of Organic Chemistry
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043768
Course Description
A one-semester course designed to advance students' knowledge of the central principles of organic chemistry, including the structure, properties, and reactivity of organic compounds and materials. Emphasis will be placed on the influence of structure on the mechanistic underpinnings of chemical reactivity and the prevalence of organic compounds in the natural and anthropogenic world.
