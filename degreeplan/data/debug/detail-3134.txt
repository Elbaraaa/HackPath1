CHEM410 - Advanced Inorganic Chemistry
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043630
Course Description
Aspects of modern inorganic chemistry including symmetry and structure, group theory and bonding, spectroscopic and magnetic properties, and reactions of transition metal compounds. Selected topics and new directions in inorganic chemistry are also discussed.
