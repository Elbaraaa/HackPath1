CHEM501A - Instrumental Analysis
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
010607
Course Description
A basic course in measurement science, intended to provide the student with an overall view of modern analytical chemistry and the instrumental methods of analysis used throughout industrial laboratories and research environments. Graduate-level requirements include a separate discussion section and separate exams.
