CHEM527 - Analytical Separations
Chemistry and Biochemistry  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
010640
Course Description
This course covers the fundamentals and applications in modern analytical separation science. Emphasis is given to important foundations and recent developments in quantitative and qualitative separations of complex samples, including, advanced chromatographic methods, electrophoretic methods and other approaches.
