Chinese  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Study of advanced modern (Mandarin) Chinese through readings in modern literature. for non-native speakers. Native speakers may not take this course.
Min Units
3
Max Units
3
Repeatable for Credit
No
Grading Basis
GRD - Regular Grades A, B, C, D, E
Career
Undergraduate
Course Requisites
Students who have not taken CHN 301 and CHN 302 at the University of Arizona must take a placement exam. Grade of B or higher required in CHN 302. CHN 301 and CHN 302 are prerequisite to CHN 417.
(CHN 415 and CHN 417 can be taken in either order).
