CHN515 - Advanced Modern Chinese
Chinese  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
010817
Course Description
Study of advanced modern (Mandarin) Chinese through readings in social science texts for non-native speakers. Native speakers may not take this course. Graduate-level requirements include more translations and additional reading.
Min Units
3
Max Units
3
Repeatable for Credit
No
Grading Basis
GRD - Regular Grades A, B, C, D, E
Career
Graduate
Course Requisites
Grade of B or higher required in CHN 301 and CHN 302 or consent of instructor. Students who have not taken CHN 301 and CHN 302 at the University of Arizona must take a placement test exam.
