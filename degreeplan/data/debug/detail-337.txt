AED497F - Structured English Immersion Teaching Methodology
Agricultural Education  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
035886
Course Description
Applied practice in sheltered content instruction for English language learners within Arizona Agriscience programs. Topics include: lesson plan development, sheltered teaching strategies, first and second language acquisition theory and the Sheltered Instruction Observation Protocol (SIOP) model. This course is designed to meet and fulfill the LRC 417 requirements.
