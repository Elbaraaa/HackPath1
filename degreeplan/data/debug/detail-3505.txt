CMM401 - Human Gross Anatomy
Cellular & Molecular Medicine  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
036616
Course Description
This course series is an intensive, dissection-based survey of the gross structure of the human body. CMM 401/501 (Summer Session I) will cover the Upper Extremity, Head, Neck, Back, Thorax, Abdomen, Pelvis and Lower Extremity. The course is open to upper-level undergrads and graduate students with instructor permission. Grades will be based on a midterm practical and a final practical and written exam. Students taking the course for graduate credit will also have an oral and written presentation requirement.
