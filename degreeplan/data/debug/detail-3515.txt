Cellular & Molecular Medicine  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This online, 5-week course will provide students in the pre-health science professions (Medicine, Pharmacy, Dentistry, Veterinary Medicine, Nursing, Public Health) as well as students planning a career in biomedical research with a background in the application of the genome sciences to medicine with a focus on the diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment of human diseases
