Agricultural Education  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Social and economic values of career and technical education, federal laws, state policies, and administration; theories and principles, with special reference to programs in the secondary school. Graduate-level requirements include a position paper on a major philosophy of education worth 100 points.
