Course Description
This course will be the first course in a two course sequence offered in partnership with Minerva. Students who take this course will be exposed to unique learning opportunities that center on problem solving in a complex health care environment. The course is intended to provide broad skills to all pre-health students. This course is the first in a sequence of two courses
