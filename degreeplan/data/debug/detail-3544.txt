CMM503 - Human Molecular Genetics Basics
Cellular & Molecular Medicine  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042295
Course Description
This course is intended to provide a background in basic human molecular genetics for students in the pre-health science professions (e.g., Medicine, Pharmacy, Dentistry, Veterinary Medicine, Nursing, Public Health) as well as for students planning a career in biomedical research to help ensure success in their professional training programs. The course can also serve as a gateway for graduate and undergraduate students who need additional background before proceeding with other advanced courses in clinical genetics and genomics.
