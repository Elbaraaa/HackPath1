CMM559 - Principles of Clinical Reasoning
Cellular & Molecular Medicine  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040722
Course Description
This course is intended as an introduction to clinical reasoning for students planning careers in the pre-health professions (Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Public Health, etc.). The course will provide an introduction to the process of clinical reasoning. With an early understanding of this process, pre-health students will be able to better integrate and employ the knowledge base they're developing into the eventual skills of diagnosis and management.
