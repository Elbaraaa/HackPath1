AED585 - Teaching Psychomotor Skills in Laboratory Sciences
Agricultural Education  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005191
Course Description
Methods and procedures in teaching psychomotor operational skills, conducting demonstrations, providing for student and teacher safety, sequencing skills activities, providing and organizing agricultural education facilities, including micro-teaching demon Graduate-level requirements include additional assigned readings, demonstrations, lesson presentations, and a position paper.
