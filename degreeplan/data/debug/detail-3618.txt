CMM624 - Advanced Genetic Counseling Skills
Cellular & Molecular Medicine  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042214
Course Description
This two-credit hour course will cover advanced genetic counseling concepts, such as genetics in public health and social policy, psychosocial issues such as confrontation/resistance and crisis intervention, foundations of family theory, self-disclosure, ethical considerations, and exploring the aspects of creating appropriate educational materials.
