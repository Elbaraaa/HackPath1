Cellular & Molecular Medicine  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
The course will cover a few medical genetic disorders in depth, with different topics each year. Clinical presentation, pathophysiology, genetic mechanisms and biochemical features will be considered. Readings will come mainly from the primary biomedical literature.
