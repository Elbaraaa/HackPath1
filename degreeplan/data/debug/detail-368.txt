AED597A - Workshop for Agricultural Educators
Agricultural Education  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005196
Course Description
Course includes various topics that contain the practical application of theoretical learning within a group setting, and involving an exchange of ideas and practical methods, skills, and principles. Each workshop is designed to assist high school Agricultural Educators with their curriculum, as well as to help them stay up-to-date on current agricultural issues.
