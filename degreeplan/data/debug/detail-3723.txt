COMM524 - Media and Politics in America
Communication  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011172
Course Description
Survey of field; media in political campaigns; media coverage of leaders, issues and institutions; leadership strategies to influence media. Graduate-level requirements include producing a 15 to 20 page research paper involving the application of two major, competing theories to a study of nightly network news.
