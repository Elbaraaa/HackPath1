Public Health, Col of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course analyzes how public health and nutrition are interrelated including factors that influence the quality of each. This course analyzes basic public health education for improving maternal, infant and child nutrition. Provides fundamental understanding of how nutrition can be used to prevent and alleviate illnesses in children. This course provides an overview of the health concerns and nutrition-related problems that affect mothers, infants and children.
