CPH325 - Dietary Assessment in Public Health Practice
Public Health, Col of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042448
Course Description
This course analyzes the role of dietary assessment in the prevention and treatment of chronic diseases. This course provides essential understanding of the impact of nutritional assessment on Healthy People 2030 objectives. This course provides an overview of how technology is changing dietary intake data collection. This course will expound on public health communication and interviewing skills for nutrition counseling.
