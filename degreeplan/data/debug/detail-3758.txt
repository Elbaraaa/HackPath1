Public Health, Col of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
In this course, students will explore the effects of climate and climate change on health. Over the course, content moves from the fundamental science of climate change and physiology, current and predicted effects, to the framing and communication of climate health impacts. Students highlight their knowledge through course deliverables including drafting white papers, peer lecture, and story maps.
