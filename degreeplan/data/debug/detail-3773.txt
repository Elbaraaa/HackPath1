Public Health, Col of  Graduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
This course introduces students to the challenges and opportunities of improving the lives of older adults through the perspective of multiple disciplines. Students will gain a holistic view of the many ways a life-course can be impacted and how interdisciplinary work is critical for change.
