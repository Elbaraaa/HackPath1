Medicine, College of, Tucson  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040937
Course Description
Principles of Clinical Research II will provide knowledge and practice on various aspects of clinical research for students and fellows in a variety of subspecialties. Students must demonstrate knowledge of established and evolving clinical research practices, conduct, and emerging trends. Students must have a working understanding of clinical research methodology, study design, preparation of applications for funding and/or approval of clinical research protocols, and critical literature review in clinical research. Upon completion students should be effective in pursuing clinical research activities and teaching both individuals and groups of learners in clinical research settings, classrooms, lectures, and seminars.
