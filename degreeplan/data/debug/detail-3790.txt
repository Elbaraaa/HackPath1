CRL102 - Elementary Language Study II
Critical Languages Program  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011544
Course Description
Builds on the foundation of CRL 101 by expanding communicative proficiency in the language, listening comprehension, and basic grammatical accuracy in practical contexts through guided self-instructional methods and weekly intensive, small-group tutorials. Language specified in course section number. See clp.arizona.edu for specific course learning outcomes. Counts towards CLP Minor and Language Requirement. Students with prior knowledge of or experience with the language being taught should contact the Critical Languages Program to arrange a placement test. (Prerequisite CRL 101 or consent of the CLP Director).
