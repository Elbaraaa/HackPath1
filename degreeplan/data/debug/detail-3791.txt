CRL197A - Elementary Language Skills Practice I
Critical Languages Program  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011545
Course Description
The practical application of theoretical learning within a group setting and involving an exchange of ideas and practical methods, skills, and principles. This flexible workshop format allows for targeted practice in elementary language skills, ideal for those seeking extra reinforcement or intensive summer study. Language specified in course section number. See clp.arizona.edu for specific course learning outcomes. Counts towards CLP Minor. Students with prior knowledge of or experience with the language being taught should contact the Critical Languages Program to arrange a placement test.
