CRL201 - Intermediate Language Study I
Critical Languages Program  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011546
Course Description
Continuing study of the language with emphasis on its spoken aspects utilizing intensive preparations with biweekly reviews. Students begin to engage in longer conversations, read short authentic texts, and explore more detailed aspects of grammar and sentence construction. Language specified in course section number. See clp.arizona.edu for specific course outcomes. Counts towards CLP Minor and Language Requirement. Students with prior knowledge of or experience with the language being taught should contact the Critical Languages Program to arrange a placement test. (Prerequisite CRL 102 or consent of the CLP Director).
