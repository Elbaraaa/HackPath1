CRL202 - Intermediate Language Study II
Critical Languages Program  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011547
Course Description
Continuing study of the language with an emphasis on its spoken aspects utilizing intensive preparations with biweekly reviews. Focuses on strengthening the ability to discuss familiar topics, understand spoken discourse at a faster pace, and write brief compositions. Language specified in course section number. See clp.arizona.edu for specific course learning outcomes. Counts towards CLP Minor and Language Requirement. Students with prior knowledge of or experience with the language being taught should contact the Critical Languages Program to arrange a placement test. (Prerequisite CRL 201 or consent of the CLP Director).
