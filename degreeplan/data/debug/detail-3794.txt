CRL297A - Intermediate Language Skills Practice II
Critical Languages Program  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011548
Course Description
The practical application of theoretical learning within a group setting and involving an exchange of ideas and practical methods, skills, and principles. This flexible workshop format enables further language development in an adaptable format; often used to address specific learning goals or to prepare for transition into advanced coursework. Language specified in course section number. See clp.arizona.edu for specific course learning outcomes. Counts towards CLP Minor. Students with prior knowledge of or experience with the language being taught should contact the Critical Languages Program to arrange a placement test. (Prerequisite CRL 102 or consent of the CLP Director).
