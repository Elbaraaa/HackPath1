CRL302 - Advanced Language Study II
Critical Languages Program  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011550
Course Description
Continuation of CRL 301 with further emphasis on reading and writing. Students refine accuracy and fluency across all skills, especially written expression and comprehension of detailed authentic materials, including idiomatic language. Language specified in course section number. See clp.arizona.edu for specific course learning outcomes. Counts towards CLP Minor. Students with prior knowledge of or experience with the language being taught should contact the Critical Languages Program to arrange a placement test. (Prerequisite CRL 301 or consent of the CLP Director).
