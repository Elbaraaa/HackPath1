CRL397A - Advanced Language Study
Critical Languages Program  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011551
Course Description
The practical application of theoretical learning within a group setting and involving an exchange of ideas and practical methods, skills, and principles. This course is ideal for students preparing for study abroad, professional use, or language proficiency certification. Language specified in course section number. Counts towards CLP Minor. Students with prior knowledge of or experience with the language being taught should contact the Critical Languages Program to arrange a placement test. (Prerequisite CRL 302 or consent of the CLP Director).
