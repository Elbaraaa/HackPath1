AED697C - Workshop on Teaching at the College Level
Agricultural Education  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005233
Course Description
Workshop that deals with the practical application of teaching/learning theories at the college level as they relate to instructional methodologies, strategies, and planning. This will include instructional objectives, content organization, and assessment of learning experiences. The workshop will involve the exchange of ideas and will focus on practical methods, skills and principles.
