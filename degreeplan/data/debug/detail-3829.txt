Computer Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This undergraduate seminar studies special topics in Computer Science within a small group format. Through selected readings, presentations, and group discussions, students will take a discussion-based learning approach. Specific topics will be determined by current topics of interest by the instructor and in special cases, the students.
