CSC547 - Green Computing
Computer Science  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
035942
Course Description
This course covers fundamental principles of energy management faced by designers of hardware, operating systems, and data centers. We will explore basic energy management option in individual components such as CPUs, network interfaces, hard drives, memory. We will further present the energy management policies at the operating system level that consider performance vs. energy saving tradeoffs. Finally we will consider large scale data centers where energy management is done at multiple layers from individual components in the system to shutting down entries subset of machines. We will also discuss energy generation and delivery and well as cooling issues in large data centers. In addition to the assignments listed for undergraduates, Graduate-level requirements include a detailed review of the three recent conference publications dealing with energy efficiency.
