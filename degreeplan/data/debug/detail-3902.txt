Computer Science  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041274
Course Description
The goal of this graduate seminar course is to learn more about research in the general field of computer systems. In this course, we will read and review research papers on computer systems. We will also learn how to do research in computer science by reading, evaluating, presenting, and conducting a research project in computer systems. Specific topics to be determined by current literature and faculty and student interest.
