CTS590A - InnoVention and Entrepreneurship in Medicine
Medicine, College of, Tucson  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
The course is directed toward acquiring the basic methodologies for inventing and developing ideas to solve problems of healthcare delivery systems through technology. Emphasis on medical devices and mobile personalized medicine will be approached from the perspective of design, manufacturing, user interface and regulatory compliance for market deployment.
