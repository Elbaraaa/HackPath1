CTS595C - Responsible Conduct of Research
Medicine, College of, Tucson  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039000
Course Description
The course addresses principles underlying the practice of biomedical, behavioral and clinical sciences, including collaboration, conflict of interest, data acquisition, management, sharing and ownership, human subjects protection, laboratory animal welfare, mentoring, peer-review, publication, and ethical responsibilities of scientists.
