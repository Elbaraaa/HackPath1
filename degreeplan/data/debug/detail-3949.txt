General

Course Description
Clinical Grand Rounds are weekly seminar series in which noted researchers, including nationally prominent experts, are invited to provide overviews of cutting edge clinical developments, research and medical education in a given specialty area. Grand Rounds is a comprehensive look at the entire spectrum of information and decision making required to properly address a specific clinical problem. Students in CTS 696A regularly attend a series of Grand Rounds presentations to augment their knowledge and spur new research questions or directions.
