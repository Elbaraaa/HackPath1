Medicine, College of, Tucson  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
The course provides for the development and exchange of scholarly information in a group session. It offers a collaborative forum for students and researchers to present their ongoing research, receive constructive feedback from peers and faculty, and engage in thoughtful discussion around experimental strategies, challenges, and interpretation of results.
