CTS699A - Clinical Problem Solving
Medicine, College of, Tucson  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041330
Course Description
This course is designed to give the student an overview as how clinical issues are addressed and managed. Students will be assigned to specific and approved sites by the course coordinator. This course will enable the students to observe and understand the complexity of clinical management and the ability to interact with healthcare profession. It is expected that the student should be able to integrate these elements into pharmacotherapeutic management strategies and thus have a deeper understand as how to maximize clinical outcomes while minimizing adverse events.
