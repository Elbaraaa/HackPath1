CYBV226 - Networking Fundamentals
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043666
Course Description
This course will provide students with a solid foundation in networking principles and technologies by introducing computer network architectures and network models, fundamental concepts, protocols, and technologies related to wired, wireless, and cloud-enabled networks. Additionally, the course introduces the fundamentals of network security and use of tools and techniques associated with network troubleshooting and maintenance.
