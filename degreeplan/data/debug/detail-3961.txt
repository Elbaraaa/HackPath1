CYBV250 - Cybersecurity First Principles
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043180
Course Description
CYBV250 will provide students with an overview of the First Principles of Cybersecurity. Students will be presented with the fundamental truths of cybersecurity and how to leverage them as the foundational building blocks when designing a cybersecurity program. Students will investigate and analyze case studies to understand the current cybersecurity landscape and how to use the atomic cybersecurity first principle to pursue defensive postures that have the greatest probability of success.
