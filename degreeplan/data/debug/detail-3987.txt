CYBV435 - Cyber Threat Intelligence
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039070
Course Description
Equips students with a holistic understanding of how cyber attacks and vulnerabilities, the threat landscape, and the intelligence process synergize to define the evolving field of Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI). Students will learn to collect, analyze, and apply intelligence about an adversary's motivations, intentions, and methods through industry-standard frameworks and practical exercises. The course enables students to align cybersecurity resources effectively and protect critical assets within various enterprise architectures by leveraging real-world scenarios and interactive activities.
