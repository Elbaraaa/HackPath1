CYBV440 - Digital Espionage
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041264
Course Description
CYBV440 teaches students about theft of state secrets for political or economic reasons. It will provide students with a comprehensive overview of the concepts, tactics, techniques adversaries use to steal secrets. Students will analyze case studies to become familiar with how to detect, identify, and mitigate digital espionage operations and the actors who conduct them.
