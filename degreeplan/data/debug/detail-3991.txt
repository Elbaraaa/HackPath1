CYBV442 - Regulatory Limitations and Controls for Cyber Operations
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041895
Course Description
CYBV 442 will provide students with an overview of how to design effective cyber policies while taking into account modern legal considerations across major industries. Students will be presented with governance frameworks, the cybersecurity policy life cycle, and how to write formal policies. Students will design an information security plan (ISP) that addresses key aspects of the organization's response to regulation.
