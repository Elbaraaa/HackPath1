CYBV443 - Legal and Privacy Constraints in Cyber Operations
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041830
Course Description
CYBV 443 will provide students with an understanding of the complex relationship between law and cyber operations. Cyber Security, legal compliance, and privacy are critical in the protection of public and private sector infrastructure, intellectual property, and other sensitive data. Students walk away from this course with an understanding of how to build numerous information security and privacy responses into the daily operations of an organization to protect the business and meet legal requirements.
