CYBV454 - Malware Threats & Analysis
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
039593
Course Description
Provides students a methodology to perform safely static and dynamic analysis of software of potentially unknown origin, including obfuscated malware, and to understand fully the software's functionality and specifications. Students will use hands-on labs and exercises to examine the fundamental principles of malware analysis and software reverse engineering.
