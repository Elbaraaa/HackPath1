CYBV460 - Principles of Zero Trust Networks
Applied Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
041227
Course Description
CYBV 460 will provide students with an overview of the fundamentals of Zero Trust Networks (ZTN). Students will understand the most effective methodologies used by leading companies and cyber professionals to design and implement a ZTN. Students will use interactive exercises to become familiar with the design concepts including Software Defined Networks (SDN) and how to leverage SDN and mutually authenticated Transport Layer Security (TLS) to create a scalable and robust ZTN.
