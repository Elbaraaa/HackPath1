CSC144 - Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science I
Computer Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042000
Course Description
The first of a two-course sequence introducing mathematical concepts for Computer Science. Topics include: propositional and predicate logic; proofs; sets, matrices, relations, and functions; integers and modular arithmetic.
