CYBV692 - Directed Research
Applied Science  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043332
Course Description
Individual research under the guidance of a faculty member. The research topic should be relevant to graduate program competencies. Students will propose a research plan that will include a problem statement, relevant literature, proposed research methods, expected outcome(s), schedule of activities, and a meeting schedule.
