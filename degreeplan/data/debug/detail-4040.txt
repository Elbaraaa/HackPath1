DATA361 - Elements of Statistics using Calculus
Mathematics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Introduces statistical inference and data science. Data collection, experimental design, and statistical techniques are taught using real data sets. Coding allows students to explore data analysis, data cleaning and visualization. Students who complete this course will be prepared to teach high school level statistics and data science courses.
