DATA498D - Capstone for Data Science
Mathematics  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043877
Course Description
A culminating \"capstone\" experience that synthesizes concepts and skills for learning from data, as accumulated over the students' experience in the Data Science and Statistics & Data Science majors. We will introduce students to activities that will round out their undergraduate experience, including, concepts in reproducible research, ethics in statistical and data science practice, high performance computing, written and oral communication skills, working in teams, and professional development. Students will engage in projects and work to complete their academic portfolio.
