General

Course Description
DCTC 402 is the capstone course of the DCTC curriculum, designed to build upon the knowledge and skills developed throughout the program. This course emphasizes leadership, systems and critical thinking, and complex problem-solving, essential for your role in the DoD acquisition community. The course offers opportunities for an in-depth exploration of the DoD system, equipping you with the knowledge and skills needed to make an immediate impact.
