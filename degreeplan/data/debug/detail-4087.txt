Dance, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Emphasis will be directed toward learning and developing both technical skills an aesthetic sensibilities at a moderately sophisticated level. Appropriate for students with 4-8 semesters of prior jazz dance training. Subtle differences in movement quality, energy and rhythm will be explored.
