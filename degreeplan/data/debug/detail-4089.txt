Dance, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
Emphasis will be directed toward learning and developing both technical skills and aesthetic sensibilities at a beginning level. No previous experience in modern dance is necessary. Differences in movement quality, energy and rhythm will be explored.
