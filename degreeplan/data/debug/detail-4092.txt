DNC174 - Black Dance in the United States: History, social media, and Other Performances
Dance, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043196
Course Description
This course explores the profound influence of Black dance throughout history, examining the elements, migrations, social dances, and media performances that shape its rich tapestry. Students will engage in immersive studio experiences, ranging from traditional African Dance to present-day social media dance phenomena. By the end of the course, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the transformative power and enduring relevance of Black dance culture in American society.
