Dance, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Traditional dances from continental Africa and throughout the African diaspora will be introduced in this course. Students will learn rhythms and movements specific to the dance traditions of various ethnic groups. Oral folklore and songs will be taught as well.
