DNC201A - Pilates Mat Class I for Non-Majors
Dance, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
036487
Course Description
This course examines the basic mat work of Joseph Pilates and its development by Ron Fletcher. It will be held in the Somatics Lab in the Stevie Eller Building and fulfills non-major elective credit. The philosophies and traditions of the work are introduced and basic concepts of body mechanics relating to the exercises are discussed. This is designed for the non-dance major population with limited movement and pilates experience.
