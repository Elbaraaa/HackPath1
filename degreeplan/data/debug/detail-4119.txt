Dance, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This course examines the basic mat work of Joseph Pilates and its development by Ron Fletcher. Through participation in mat exercises, students will learn the basic mat work and basic concepts of body mechanics relating to the exercises. For majors only.
