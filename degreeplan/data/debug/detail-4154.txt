DNC495A - Teaching Methods for Dance
Dance, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011688
Course Description
This course is for dance majors and dance graduate students who are interested in developing skills in teaching ballet, jazz and modern dance techniques at the beginning level. The course is designed as a workshop where students will have an opportunity to directly apply information in a classroom setting. Dance injury prevention and the application of music to the technique class are also explored.
