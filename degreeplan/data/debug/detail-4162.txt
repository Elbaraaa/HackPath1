Dance, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Course surveys dance forms in various cultures of the world. The course combines elements of the disciplines of history and ethnology as it focuses on the evolution of dance forms and their derivative styles in social context. Graduate-level requirements include a research paper.
