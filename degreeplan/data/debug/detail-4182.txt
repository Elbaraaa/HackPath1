DNC596A - Critical Issues in Dance
Dance, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
011730
Course Description
The purpose of this class is to identify current topics in dance, and to research those topics using traditional and contemporary research methods. Students will develop a research topic and write a research paper, an abstract, create an annotated bibliography and make a 20-minute power point presentation on their topic.
