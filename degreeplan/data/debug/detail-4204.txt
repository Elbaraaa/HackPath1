DPT781 - Elective Advanced Pediatrics
Health Professions, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043124
Course Description
The elective options allow students to develop skills and knowledge in areas of interest and in which they might subsequently wish to pursue clinical specialization. These three-week modules, students select four, will be opportunities for comprehensive education with skilled specialists and development of the necessary scientific basis and clinical and personal skills for the settings. Students may have the opportunity to participate in one of these more specialized settings for their final terminal clinical experience. In Advanced Pediatrics students will further develop their skills working with the pediatric clients while gaining greater knowledge and skills in the management of these individuals including equipment and life transitions.
