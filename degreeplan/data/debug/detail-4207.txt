Health Professions, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043148
Course Description
The elective options allow students to develop skills and knowledge in areas of interest and in which they might subsequently wish to pursue clinical specialization. These three-week modules, students select four, will be opportunities for comprehensive education with skilled specialists and development of the necessary scientific basis and clinical and personal skills for the settings. Students may have the opportunity to participate in one of these more specialized settings for their final terminal clinical experience. Oncology allows students to develop their understanding of the critical role PT can take for individuals with an oncology diagnosis. The newest clinical specialty is developing cutting edge work to show the benefits of PT for clients of all ages and students will also develop the unique personal skills required to successfully work with this population.
