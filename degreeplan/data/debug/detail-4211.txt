DPT788 - Elective, The sports physical therapist
Health Professions, Sch of  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043145
Course Description
The elective options allow students to develop skills and knowledge in areas of interest and in which they might subsequently wish to pursue clinical specialization. These three-week modules, students select four, will be opportunities for comprehensive education with skilled specialists and development of the necessary scientific basis and clinical and personal skills for the settings. Students may have the opportunity to participate in one of these more specialized settings for their final terminal clinical experience. In Sports physical therapy, the student will learn the unique demands of the athlete with respect to injury, prevention, and rehabilitation. The student will also learn to work and juggle the demands of the multiple team members involved with the athlete and the unique psychological demands on the individual.
