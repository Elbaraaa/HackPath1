ECE220 - Basic Circuits
Electrical & Computer Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012088
Course Description
Elementary, transient and sinusoidal analysis of linear circuits with laboratory. Topics include: passive sign convention, mesh and node analysis, Thevenin equivalents, op-amps, capacitance, inductance, first and second order circuits, phasors, impedance, transformers, PSpice simulation software.
