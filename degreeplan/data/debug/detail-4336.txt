ECE320A - Circuit Theory
Electrical & Computer Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012110
Course Description
Electric circuits in the frequency domain, using sinusoidal steady-state, Laplace and Fourier methods; single-phase and three-phase power; time domain methods and convolution; transformed networks; natural frequencies; poles and zeros; two-port network parameters; and Fourier series analysis.
