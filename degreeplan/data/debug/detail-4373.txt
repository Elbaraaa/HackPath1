ECE450 - Analog Integrated Circuits
Electrical & Computer Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012165
Course Description
Nonswitching aspects of analog integrated circuits using bipolar or CMOS technologies. Biasing, DC signal behavior, small behavior. Emphasis on use of physical reasoning, identification of circuit functions, and use of suitable approximations to facilitate understanding and analysis.
