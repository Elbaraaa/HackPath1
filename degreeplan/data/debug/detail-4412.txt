ECE503B - Software Tools for Photonics
Optical Sciences, Col  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
035533
Course Description
A brief/concise course description: Many photonics software tools are available as off the shelf modeling programs, encompassing both active and passive photonics components. These products are now in use by a wide number of telecoms companies and laboratories around the world, helping to develop the next generation of telecoms components and systems. Experience in modeling enables the development of custom solutions for specialized industry telecommunication and photonics requirements
