ECE546 - Semiconductor Processing
Materials Science & Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Silicon and compound semiconductor materials preparation, bulk crystal growth, wafering, epitaxial growth, photolithography, doping, ion implantation, etching, oxidation, metallization, silicon and compound semiconductor device processing. Graduate-level requirements include an additional research paper requiring independent research.
