ECE559 - Fundamentals of Optics for Electrical Engineers
Electrical & Computer Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012300
Course Description
Introduction to diffraction and 2D Fourier optics, geometrical optics, paraxial systems, third order aberrations, Gaussian beam propagation, optical resonators, polarization, temporal and spatial coherence, optical materials and nonlinear effects, electro-optic modulators. Applications to holography, optical data storage, optical processing, neural nets, associative memory optical interconnects. Graduate-level requirements include different exam questions and/or grading.
