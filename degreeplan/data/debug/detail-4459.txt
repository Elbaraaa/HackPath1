ECE563C - Introduction to Extended Reality (XR)
Electrical & Computer Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043804
Course Description
This course introduces students to the field of Extended Reality (XR), focusing on designing and developing interactive augmented and virtual reality experiences. Students will explore fundamental concepts of spatial computing, XR technologies, and their applications using tools like Unity and C#. By the end of the course, students will have hands-on experience in creating XR applications and an understanding of the theoretical concepts that power these technologies.
