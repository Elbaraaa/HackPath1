ECE588 - Microwave Engineering II: Active Circuit Design
Electrical & Computer Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012345
Course Description
Planar active microwave circuits, diode and transistor characteristics, mixers, amps, oscillators, and frequency multipliers. Students will design circuits with CAD tools, fabricate in clean room, and measure performance in the lab. Graduate-level requirements include extra problems involving more challenging concepts and indepth knowledge of the material.
