Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
This upper-division biology course examines human genetics and evolution with emphasis on inheritance, disease, and human evolutionary history.

Course Requisites
ECOL 182L Introductory Biology II Lab, ECOL 182R Introductory Biology II lecture, and ECOL 335 Evolutionary Biology are recommended. A brief review is offered the first week of class.
