Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Introduction to the study of genomics and its relevance to molecular, cellular and organismal biology, human health and disease. This course integrates readings and discussions of current topics, and exercises that apply web-based computational tools for genome analysis.
