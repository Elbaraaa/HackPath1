Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Basic processes and patterns of evolution: natural selection, evolutionary genetics, the analysis of adaptation, the phylogeny of life, the fossil record, molecular evolution, macroevolution. Mandatory discussion session to meet once per week.
