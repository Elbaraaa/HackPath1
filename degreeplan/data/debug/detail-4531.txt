ECOL340 - Evolution of Plant Form and Function
Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012465
Course Description
The diversity in the interaction between the way organisms are put together (\"form\") and how they work (\"function\") in response to environmental challenges. The physiological mechanisms responsible for the flow and transformation of energy and materials within organisms and among organisms framed in the larger context of ecological processes.
