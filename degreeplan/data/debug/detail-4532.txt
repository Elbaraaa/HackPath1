ECOL345 - Biodiversity and the Tree of Life
Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012466
Course Description
Introduction to the diversity of life on Earth, using phylogenetic history as an organizing principle. Course emphasizes key evolutionary innovations in eukaryotes, such as multicellularity, parasitism, symbiosis, and complex morphological novelties.
