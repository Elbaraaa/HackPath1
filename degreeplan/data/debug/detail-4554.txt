ECOL409 - Evolution of Infectious Disease
Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012493
Course Description
Causes and consequences of evolutionary change in pathogens. Evolutionary principles, vertebrate immunity, molecular epidemiology, evolution of virulence, evolution of antimicrobial resistance, predicting epidemics, impacts of infectious disease on host evolution, HIV evolution.
