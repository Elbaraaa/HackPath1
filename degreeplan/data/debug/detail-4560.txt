ECOL418 - Spatio-Temporal Ecology
Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
038411
Course Description
Population growth and species interactions in spatially and temporally varying environments. Meta populations and communities. The scale transition, the storage effect, nonlinear competitive variance, fitness-density covariance, disturbance, competition-colonization tradeoffs.
