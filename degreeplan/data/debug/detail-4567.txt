ECOL428L - Microbial Genetics Laboratory
Plant Pathology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Laboratory associated with lecture course on Prokaryotic gene structure and function; methods of gene transfer and mapping, DNA structure, replication, transcription, and translation. Hands-on computer analysis of DNA sequences and gene cloning strategies. Principles of regulation of gene expression. Biology of plasmids and bacteriophages.
