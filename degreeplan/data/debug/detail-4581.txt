ECOL451 - Drosophila: Model System for Evolution
Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
012543
Course Description
Drosophila is the most important eukaryotic model system for comparative studies in biology. We will explore several aspects of its ecology, genetics and evolution, making connections to general concepts of biology and applied research. The course will combine lectures with hands-on activities (lab and computer) and field trips.
