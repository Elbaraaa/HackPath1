ECOL464 - Sonoran Desert Discovery
Ecology & Evolutionary Biology  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
034243
Course Description
In Sonoran Desert Discovery, enrolled students will teach biological, ecological, and ecosystem principles (Biosphere 1) to school groups and the public (kindergarten to retiree) at Biosphere 2 and other Tucson venues. Students will build inquiry-based workshops (on campus and in small groups) to lead for the public. Students will develop public-speaking skills and their own effective teaching style while learning about Sonoran desert ecology and natural history.
