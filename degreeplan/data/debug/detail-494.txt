AGTM330 - Turf and Landscape Technology
Agricultural Education  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
006077
Course Description
The basic scientific principles and skills of construction, operation, and maintenance in turf, landscape and urban agricultural equipment. Provides student with laboratory experiences in machinery, sprinkler and drip irrigation installation, operation and maintenance, chemical application systems, and hardscaping.
