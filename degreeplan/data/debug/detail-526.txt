App Inter Arts Res GIDP  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This independent study course is designed for students to pursue independent research on topics related to a student's research agenda. Students will work closely with a faculty supervisor to design the goals of the independent study. Supervision should come from a faculty member affiliated with the AIAR GIDP.
