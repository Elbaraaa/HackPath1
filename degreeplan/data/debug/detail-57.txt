CSC493 - Internship
Computer Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
009857
Course Description
Specialized work on an individual basis, consisting of training and practice in actual service in a technical, business, or governmental establishment. Such work must be approved and supervised by a Computer Science faculty member. Available to Computer Science majors. May be repeated: Between C SC 293 and 493, a total of 6 units of internship may be earned.
