Agricultural Education  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042154
Course Description
In this course, students will learn the practical process of establishing commercial entities, as well as the process of generating high quality small business grant applications. Students generally regard themselves as \"job seekers\". This course is designed to encourage students to investigate what it might take to become a \"job creator\". It is assumed that participating students have little to no experience in business creation processes.
