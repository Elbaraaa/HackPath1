AME211 - Computer-Aided Drafting and Manufacturing
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
040664
Course Description
The aim of this course is to provide the students with fundamentals in mechanical drafting and how it relates to manufacturing (CNC and additive manufacturing) and modern computational tools such as finite element analysis. SolidWorks will be used as the main learning and practice tool.
