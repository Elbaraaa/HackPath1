Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This course provides hands-on composite design considerations, material handling, safety and manufacturing techniques. The composite engineering life cycle will be demonstrated and then performed by the student while focusing on material safety.
