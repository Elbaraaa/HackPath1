AME320 - Aerodynamics
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005337
Course Description
Basic equations and their approximation; potential flow theory; fundamentals of airfoil and wing theory; viscous and compressibility effects; an introduction to compressible flows; application to aerodynamics of wings and bodies.
