AME324C - Aerospace Structures
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
038734
Course Description
Application of principles of mechanics to the structural analysis of aerospace components. Topics covered include analysis of stress and strain, constitutive relations, plane problems of elasticity, torsion, bending, elastic stability, energy methods, and finite element methods.
