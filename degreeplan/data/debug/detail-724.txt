AME324L - Mechanics of Materials Laboratory
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005343
Course Description
Characterization of engineering materials for stress-strain relations, deformation, hardness, strength, fracture, and cyclic fatigue. The course comprises of hands-on experience with instruments, specimens, recording and interpretation of data, and formal engineering report writing.
