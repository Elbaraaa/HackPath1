Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This laboratory introduces students to experimental vibration testing using modern measurement techniques and hands-on experiments. It also exposes students to real-time control experiments and data acquisition based on experimental testbeds.
