AME407 - Structural Mechanics
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043224
Course Description
The course covers scientific principles and methods of structural mechanics for analysis of problems arising in mechanical and aerospace structural design. Course topics include fundamentals of three-dimensional theory of elasticity; stress-strain relationships; two-dimensional problems of elasticity; material failure criteria; plastic behavior of materials; plates.
