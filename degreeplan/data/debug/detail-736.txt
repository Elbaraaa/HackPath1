AME420 - Aerospace Conceptual Design
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
038712
Course Description
Phases of design; take-off weight, Breguet method; sizing to stall speed requirements; sizing to landing distance; sizing to climb requirements; maneuvering; cruise requirements; landing distance sizing; summary of matching results; selection of overall configuration; aircraft of unusual configurations. Students form teams to work on industry-sponsored projects. Preliminary design review by end of the semester.
