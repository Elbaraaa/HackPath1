AME433 - Intermediate Fluid Mechanics
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005399
Course Description
Basic equations governing fluid motion. Fundamental solutions to Navier Stokes equations, principles of lubrication theory, elementary potential flow theory, turbulence, boundary layers, separation and drag, one-dimensional compressible flow, shock waves, measurement techniques.
