AME444 - Applied Thermodynamics
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005408
Course Description
This course is envisioned for interested seniors and graduate students (and interested working engineers) as a means to round out their understanding of the application of the sophisticated thermodynamic principles of availability and entropy generation minimization to systems of practical and commercial importance, including cryogenic refrigerators and liquifiers.
