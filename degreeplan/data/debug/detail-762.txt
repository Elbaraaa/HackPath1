Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Classification and characteristics of composite materials; mechanical behavior of composite materials, micro- and macro-mechanical behavior of laminae; mechanical behavior of laminates; mechanical behavior of short fiber composites.
