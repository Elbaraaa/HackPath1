Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
Introductory aspects of nuclear engineering, including the nuclear physics of nuclear reactors, interaction of radiation with matter, nuclear reactor theory, reactor heat removal, radiation protection, and radiation shielding.
