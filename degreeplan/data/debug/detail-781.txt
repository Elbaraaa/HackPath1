AME520 - Aircraft Conceptual Design
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005467
Course Description
Phases of design; take-off weight, Breguet method; sizing to stall speed requirements; sizing to landing distance; sizing to climb requirements; maneuvering; cruise requirements; landing distance sizing; summary of matching results; selection of overall configuration; aircraft of unusual configurations. Students form teams to work on industry-sponsored projects. Preliminary design review by end of the semester. Students develop conceptual designs for aircraft with specified performances and figures of merit.
