AME524 - Aerospace Design Fundamentals
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
043517
Course Description
This is a design class covering theory and practice of aircraft and spacecraft conceptual systems design process. The class covers aerospace systems engineering design principles, preliminary design of aerospace subsystems, subsystem design architectures, trade studies, and introduction to design optimization.
