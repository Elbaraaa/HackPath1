AME544 - Applied Thermodynamics
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005499
Course Description
This course is envisioned for interested seniors and graduate students (and interested working engineers) as a means to round out their understanding of the application of the sophisticated thermodynamic principles of availability and entropy generation minimization to systems of practical and commercial importance, including cryogenic refrigerators and liquifiers. Graduate-level requirements include material taught, such as \"constructal theory\" and entropy generation minimization, that are generally too sophisticated to use as undergraduate material.
