AME560 - Advanced Vibration
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005515
Course Description
Analytical treatment of the vibration of continuous and distributed parameter systems such as strings, rods, and beams; finite-element and numerical methods in vibration and modal analysis; frequency domain analysis; nonlinear effects and stochastic methods.

3
Max Units
