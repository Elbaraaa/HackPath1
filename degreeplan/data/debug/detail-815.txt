AME562 - Composite Materials
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005521
Course Description
Classification and characteristics of composite materials; mechanical behavior of composite materials, micro- and macro-mechanical behavior of laminae; mechanical behavior of laminates; mechanical behavior of short fiber composites. Graduate-level requirements include an additional project on composite materials.
