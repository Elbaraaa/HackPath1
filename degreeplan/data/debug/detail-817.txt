Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
Mathematical preliminaries, motion and deformation, balance laws of mechanics, state of stress and equations of motion, constitutive equations, uniqueness, Betti-Rayleigh reciprocity theorem, applications from classical elasticity.
