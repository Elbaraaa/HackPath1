AME568 - Probabilistic Design
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
005529
Course Description
Theoretical and computational aspects for propagation of uncertainties through a model. Methods for problems with traditional random variables and random fields will be presented. These fundamental aspects will be complemented by the description of engineering applications.
