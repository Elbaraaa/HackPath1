AME586 - Microfluidics
Aerospace & Mechanical Engr  Graduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
Fundamentals of micro scale fluid mechanics: size effects; fabrication and diagnostic techniques for micro fluidic systems; pressure-driven gas flows in micro systems; electro kinetically-driven liquid flows in micro systems; micro polar liquid flows.
