Civil & Arch Engr & Mechanics  Graduate  UA - UA General

Course Description
[Usually offered every other Spring beginning 2003] Stress (acoustic wave propagation and dispersion in infinite solids and finite wave guides), application of wave propagation theory in destructive and nondestructive evaluation of materials and structures; dynamic failure behavior of materials.
