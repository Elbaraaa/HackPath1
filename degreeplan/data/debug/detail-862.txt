ANSP868A - Pediatric Anesthesia
COM Phx Ugrd Medical Education  Medicine  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This rotation will provide students interested in pursuing a residency in anesthesiology with a broad overall experience in pediatric anesthesia. The focus will be on anatomy, physiology pharmacology specific to children, and the approach to providing anesthesia in response to the differences between children and adults.
