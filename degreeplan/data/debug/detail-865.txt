Anthropology, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General

Course Description
This course introduces students to the field of medical anthropology. Medical anthropology examines how cultural, historical, economic, and political forces shape ideas about health, wellness, illness, and disease. It studies how these forces influence health disparities, healthcare seeking, health related practices and perceptions of risk, the structure of medical systems, and much more.
