ANTH307 - Ecological Anthropology
Anthropology, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID

Course Description
This course will examine how ecologies and human societies interact and impact each other. It will examine a wide range of human ecologies throughout the world and across time, including anthropogenic environmental changes on a planetary scale.
