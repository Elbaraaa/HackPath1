CSC244 - Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science II
Computer Science  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
042001
Course Description
A second course in mathematical concepts for computer science. The topics covered include recursive definitions, recurrence relations, induction, graphs, trees, combinatorics, and discrete probability.
