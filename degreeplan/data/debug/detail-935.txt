ANTH351 - To Boldly Go: Understanding Anthropology Through Science Fiction
Anthropology, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
044017
Course Description
Science fiction relies on culture to build believable worlds in film, literature, and other media. Infamous openings such as \"a mission exploring strange new worlds where no one has gone before\" signal we are about to be launched from our present reality into a universe that is fundamentally different, yet also familiar. Science fiction is an anthropological exploration of extra-terrestrial cultures, a study of the other, and a vehicle to critically examine our own cultural systems. This course takes students on a thematic journey through the fictional universes of Star Trek and other worlds to build understanding of how science fiction uses Anthropological concepts to question, explore, and clarify both their fictional worlds and the world around us. Throughout the course students will engage with anthropological methods and theories to critically analyze culture in science fiction film, television, and literature and consider how these themes connect to anthropological research.
