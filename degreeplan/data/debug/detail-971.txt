ANTH406 - Anthropology of Gender
Anthropology, Sch of  Undergraduate  UA - UA General 
General
Course ID
007017
Course Description
This course is designed to enhance students understanding of the importance of gender in structuring social relations across time and space. It will cover the theoretical foundations, history of the gender discourse, and use cross-cultural examples to address topics central to anthropology.
